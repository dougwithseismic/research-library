Launch-Cell Intent, Competition and Acquisition Economics

A purchase-intent, current-supply, venue-price, acquisition and compliance pressure test for the leading Manchester, Birmingham and Bristol launch cells.

Geography
UK; selected European and US comparators
Research date
2026-09-03
Status
Published research

The answer#

The broad format ranking was useful, but it was still one step away from a launch decision. Large category searches often mean find me a venue, studio or facility, not sell me a hosted recurring social event.

The deeper launch-cell screen changes the reading:

  1. Manchester social darts remains the best first paid pilot, but only as a beginner league or season. Manchester has observable event, league and club intent; it also has powerful one-off venue substitutes. The product must sell continuity rather than darts access.
  2. Birmingham salsa is the strongest measured repeat-demand control, not clean whitespace. salsa classes Birmingham has the same rounded average volume as the broad discovery phrase, while Havana Salsa already operates a recurring Birmingham-area product. A challenger needs a sharper audience, location, social design or partnership.
  3. Birmingham line dancing is the more interesting whitespace hypothesis, but evidence is thinner. The explicit class phrase is reportable; current active supply, facilitator cost and paid conversion are not yet sufficiently evidenced.
  4. Manchester wine tasting is commercially legible but crowded. It has event, class and corporate intent and a current £40–£60 consumer event ladder, yet cold paid search only works in the illustrative model at unusually strong conversion and repeat.
  5. Birmingham board games, pottery painting and sip-and-paint have impressive discovery demand but weak explicit event intent. All three have visible current supply. The opportunity, if any, is a new social system layered onto partner capacity—not another venue or generic workshop.
  6. Spa and residential retreats remain extensions, not wedges. Search is destination-, practitioner- and audience-led; the format carries supplier, refund, insurance and sometimes package-travel exposure before the first customer arrives.

The immediate recommendation is therefore narrower than the preceding chapter: presell one six-event Manchester darts season; run Birmingham salsa and line dancing as proposition controls; secure a partner quote for one adult pottery counter-test; do not commit retreat inventory.

The retained evidence is the explicit-city intent screen, local-geo intent screen, intent summary, launch decision register, current supply and price ledger, acquisition sensitivity and risk-and-controls register.

What this stage measured#

The national screen asked which formats are visible. The fifteen-city screen asked where those formats are relatively liquid. This stage asks a more commercial question: what is the searcher trying to buy?

Eight formats were examined in Manchester, Birmingham and Bristol across five separate intent baskets:

  • broad discovery;
  • dated event booking;
  • repeat programme, league, club or class;
  • beginner or adult positioning;
  • group, workshop or corporate use.

For every city-format-intent cell, an explicit city-modified query under the UK target was kept separate from a generic local query inside the Google Ads city target. The result is 120 explicit rows and 120 local-geo rows. The two methods overlap and are not added. Google may group close variants, and rounded or zero-return rows do not prove no demand.

This is still search evidence, not customer evidence. It does not show organic ranking difficulty, map-pack competition, obtainable clicks, booking conversion, attendance, repeat or profit.

The discovery-to-purchase gap#

The useful result is not that some large numbers became smaller. It is that the shape of intent differs by format.

These rows are separate observations, not a funnel. Dividing one by another would not produce a conversion rate or market share. The comparison is diagnostic: it tells the founder which customer language deserves a landing-page and deposit test.

It also changes the category hierarchy. Pottery painting is one of the largest broad activity signals in the entire research, but the Birmingham adult, event, class and corporate phrases are either zero-return or extremely thin in this pull. Salsa is smaller as a category but far stronger as a repeat purchase behaviour. If the aim is to build a recurring social company rather than a directory of popular activities, the second pattern is more valuable.

Current supply: validation is not whitespace#

The supply review is deliberately narrow and first-party. It proves that selected offers were live on 3 September 2026; it is not a complete competitor census and page absence is never treated as proof of no supply.

Manchester darts#

Flight Club Manchester advertises fourteen oche areas, group and work-event routes, stated capacity up to 340 and Social Darts from £10 per person. Roxy also advertises tech darts at Manchester Arndale and Deansgate. Flight Club Manchester Roxy tech darts

That is strong behavioural and price validation, but it closes the generic wedge. A new company should not build a lesser Flight Club. It should use ordinary pub capacity to own the relationships Flight Club does not necessarily own: beginner identity, rotating teams, standings, a season final and the next paid booking.

Birmingham dance#

Havana Salsa publishes weekly UK classes, a Birmingham-area route and non-London prices of £11.80 for one hour, £20 for two classes on the same night and £40.80 for a four-class block. Birmingham classes Current class prices

This is close to the proposed product, not merely an adjacent venue. Birmingham salsa should be treated as a challenger or partner test. Line dancing may have more open headroom, but the research did not establish a dependable live-supply census or price benchmark. Its uncertainty should increase the testing burden, not be called whitespace.

Birmingham games and making#

Chance & Counters offers 650+ games in Digbeth for £2.50 per person per hour off-peak or £3.50 peak, with staff who recommend and teach games. A proposed £15 hosted night therefore has to make strangers mix, remove awkwardness and create continuity; access to games and rules help are already inexpensive. Chance & Counters Birmingham

Pottery is more crowded than the broad ranking implied. Café Craft offers 150+ pieces, firing, adult and child parties, corporate workshops and off-site fulfilment. Glaze Room charges £10 studio fee plus £18–£50 for pottery and can host private groups up to 30. Coated Pottery serves solo, group and child visits in Kings Heath and fires in-house. Café Craft Glaze Room Coated Pottery

The implication is specific: do not open a studio and do not buy a kiln. Buy spare capacity, materials and firing from an operator; add adult cohort design and a return path; cancel the hypothesis if the partner margin cannot survive below near sell-out.

Brush Party provides a current Birmingham-area reference for sip-and-paint: a three-hour guided event, supplies included, food and drink bought separately, £33 for the reviewed 15 September event and a seventh-standard-event-free loyalty mechanism. Paint Away supplies another portable public, private and corporate model across Greater Manchester. Brush Party Birmingham event Paint Away

The operating model is proven; differentiation is not. A new generic themed painting night is low priority unless it brings a proprietary audience, facilitator or repeat mechanic.

Manchester wine#

Manchester Wine School publishes current introductory tastings, pairing events, masterclasses, qualifications, gifts and corporate/private services. The reviewed consumer event ladder runs from £40 to £60, while WSET examples extend from £200 to £995. Manchester Wine School

This is excellent evidence for willingness to pay and product depth. It is also evidence that generic wine education is occupied. A new tasting needs a named producer, unusual audience, distinctive cultural wrapper or a distribution advantage.

Public venue prices narrow one unknown—but do not replace quotes#

Public room rates show that the earlier fixed-cost scenarios are not absurd, but they are not supplier offers for the proposed activities.

Friends' Meeting House Manchester currently lists weekday-evening room rates from £50 for selected 25-person circle rooms, £70 for selected 26–34-person circle rooms, £190 for the Upper Hall and £210 for the Main Hall. The Birmingham Priory Rooms lists £116 evening hire for several rooms, £254 for two larger rooms and £320 for its Main Meeting House. Manchester evening hire Birmingham room-only prices

Neither page confirms that alcohol, darts, dancing, noisy music, messy craft or the required room layout is permitted. Neither price includes every facilitator, equipment, cleaning, catering, licensing, insurance or staffing cost. A signed all-in quote and responsibility schedule must replace the desk scenario before tickets go on sale.

Paid acquisition is possible only in the repeat case#

The acquisition sensitivity does not forecast campaign performance. It combines selected Google Ads historical CPC observations with the 90%-fill event scenarios. Acquisition is excluded from those direct event-cost scenarios so it can be pressure-tested separately.

For each format, the model allocates 50% of illustrative lifetime direct contribution to customer acquisition. The number of attended events per acquired customer is assumed, not observed: four for darts, six for dance and two for wine. If repeat fails, the allowable CAC collapses.

Manchester darts-event and Birmingham salsa-class search could fit the illustrative model around 5% click-to-booking—but only if the assumed repeat purchases occur and the events themselves reach 90% fill. Darts-club and wine-event routes require roughly 8% and 10% respectively. At 2% conversion, their illustrative CPAs are about £49 and £50, far above the modelled allowance.

The zero-CPC rows are not cheap channels. They are unpriceable from this evidence. The 10-search pottery row is too rounded and too small to support a forecast, even though it carries a nominal CPC.

The management conclusion is not “run Google Ads.” It is: a launch cannot rely on cold search unless full-price conversion and repeat are measured together. Partner distribution, referrals, facilitator audiences and venue lists may be more important, but their reach and economics remain unobserved.

The operating and compliance gates#

The risk register converts general obligations into pre-sale evidence. It is a management checklist, not legal or safety advice.

Trigger Minimum evidence before exposure becomes non-refundable
Any in-person event Signed responsibility split, activity-specific risk assessment, safe capacity, emergency plan, access facts and incident owner
Alcohol, dance, indoor sport or regulated entertainment Written premises-licence coverage or an accepted Temporary Event Notice where required
Food or tasting Named responsible food business, current ingredient/allergen matrix and attendee-question process
Pottery Premixed public-session materials, safety data, no dry powder or spraying, competent firing partner and breakage terms
Dated consumer ticket Clear inclusions, age/access limits, transfer/refund/reschedule terms, minimum-attendance rule and full refund liquidity
Retreat with accommodation or other travel services Specialist package-travel review, supplier contracts and applicable insolvency protection before bundling
Every format Activity-specific insurance accepted by the venue and supplier/facilitator certificates

HSE says organisers should establish safe crowd capacity, assess the actual venue and define shared responsibilities with venue management. GOV.UK says a Temporary Event Notice may be needed for licensable activities on unlicensed premises; the current route covers fewer than 500 people, lasts no more than 168 hours, costs £21 and ordinarily requires ten clear working days. HSE event safety Temporary Event Notice

Food Standards Agency guidance requires food businesses to provide allergen information and manage allergens. HSE's ceramics guidance identifies silica and other hazardous-substance risks in glaze preparation and related production tasks; using ready-made bisque, premixed products and a competent firing partner removes work from the public event but does not replace an assessment of the actual materials. Allergen guidance HSE ceramics guidance

Specific-date leisure services are generally excepted from the standard distance-contract cancellation right, but that is not permission for unclear or unfair terms and does not remove remedies when the organiser cancels or misdescribes the service. Retreats need an additional check: combining accommodation with another travel service can bring a sale within the Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations depending on the structure and facts. The exact first experiment#

Primary: Manchester beginner darts season#

Offer one fixed six-week weekday season at £18 per attended session or an economically equivalent prepaid pack. Cap the initial model at 48. Use a partner pub with enough boards, but retain the customer permission, team allocation, standings, host playbook and next-booking relationship.

The desk scenario breaks even at 26 paid attendees and generates £111.50 direct contribution at 33 attendees or £266.50 at 43, before acquisition, VAT, central overhead, founder labour, insurance, tax and failed-event losses.

Do not commit beyond the first season unless:

  • written venue and board-bank terms preserve direct break-even below 60% fill;
  • at least 26 seats are paid before the venue's non-refundable date;
  • paid attendance reaches at least 33 by event three without a permanent discount;
  • at least 30% of first-time customers place a second paid booking or pack deposit within 45 days;
  • direct contribution exceeds 20% before central costs by event four;
  • a trained non-founder host can deliver the product;
  • no severe safety or conduct issue remains unresolved.

This is a product test, not a market-share claim. The useful assets are the repeat cohort, operating data and host system—not the first busy room.

Control: Birmingham salsa versus line dancing#

Test two concrete propositions at the same intended price and comparable time/location. Both include instruction, low-anxiety rotation, protected practice and a next-block offer. Do not interpret email sign-ups as demand: use clearly refundable deposits or paid bookings.

Salsa is the demand-positive, incumbent-heavy control. Line dancing is the weaker-demand, potentially more open hypothesis. Proceed to delivery only when a signed venue/facilitator model breaks even below 65% and paid deposits clear that threshold. The key outcome is the next block, not applause after the first night.

Counter-test: Birmingham adult pottery cohort#

Request written spare-capacity or off-site terms from a studio that supplies materials and firing. Sell an adult, solo-friendly hosted cohort rather than undifferentiated painting access. With the present assumptions, at least 13 of 16 places must be paid before the partner cancellation date. If a £45 ticket cannot leave useful contribution below 75% fill, stop.

Deferred: retreat#

Validate a lower-ticket city wellness event and a named practitioner audience first. Then present a partner day retreat with exact venue, treatments, accessibility, cancellation schedule and a refundable-deposit threshold. Residential inventory is committed only after the legal, insurance, rooming and cash-exposure position is documented.

What changed in the thesis#

The 244-format score should be read as where to investigate, not what to launch. This chapter adds three filters that the broad ranking could not supply:

  • direct purchase and repeat language;
  • current supply and price depth;
  • acquisition and pre-sale survivability.

After those filters, the best format is no longer the activity with the largest search number. It is the activity where a founder can name the exact repeat product, buy capacity without owning the asset, acquire a customer within retained contribution and cancel before a weak test becomes a balance-sheet problem.

That makes Manchester beginner darts the cleanest first experiment. Birmingham dance is the best proposition comparison. Pottery is a partner counter-test. Wine is a differentiated partner series. Games and sip-and-paint require proof that the hosted layer is worth more than existing access. Spa retreats wait for an audience.

What remains unknown#

This stage still does not establish:

  • signed venue, board-bank, facilitator, studio, firing, wine or spa wholesale terms;
  • actual impression share, click-through, click-to-booking or event-level acquisition cost;
  • organic and map-pack difficulty;
  • partner list size, referral conversion or facilitator audience portability;
  • paid attendance, refund, no-show, incident and first-to-second booking rates;
  • competitor utilisation, retention, contribution or profit;
  • customer willingness to pay specifically for solo-friendly hosting and continuity;
  • whether any current package-travel, licensing, insurance or safety requirement applies to a final proposed contract and venue.

The next evidence is no longer another broad keyword pull. It is three written supplier packs, paid deposits on two sharply different propositions and one delivered six-event cohort with customer-level repeat and contribution tracking.
