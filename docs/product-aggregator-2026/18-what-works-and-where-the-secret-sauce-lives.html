What Works and Where the Secret Sauce Lives

Mechanism-level case studies of compounding evidence, compatibility, price history, trust, supplier workflow and distribution.

Geography
United Kingdom
Research date
2026-09-03
Status
Published research

What is the direct answer?#

The secret sauce is a compounding decision system.

It has three minimum ingredients:

  1. a record others do not already possess—a measured result, resolved identity, historical offer, compatibility rule, verified supplier or buyer outcome;
  2. a useful decision function—the record changes which product, bundle, seller or project is suitable;
  3. a recurrence loop—the buyer saves something, the supplier works inside the system, or each new observation improves later decisions.

The strongest operators add two more:

  1. commercial alignment—the payer receives a defined sale, lead, subscription benefit, data product or workflow rather than vague exposure;
  2. distribution portability—the asset can reach a website, email, app, API, partner or agent without becoming dependent on one search result.

An operator with only one ingredient has a feature. Two can make a useful publication. Three or more interlocking ingredients can become a company.

1. RTINGS: the method is the product#

RTINGS describes 4,870 products bought and tested, an approximately 40,000-square-foot laboratory, standardised category test benches, retained products and repeated testing. Each test bench is versioned, and scores are produced from published measurements and curves rather than a universal impression. RTINGS About Us, test benches and scoring

That creates several compounding assets:

  • a comparable measurement history;
  • a physical calibration set;
  • a public method that can be criticised and improved;
  • a correction and retest process;
  • category-specific thresholds connecting a measurement to a user need.

The article is a view over that system. If the prose disappeared, the measurements, methods and retained units would still be useful.

RTINGS also exposes the funding tension. Its March 2026 membership revision put most full test results across 28 categories behind membership and argued that member funding better aligns incentives than affiliate dependence. That is the operator's stated interpretation, not independent proof that the new model will grow. It demonstrates the real strategic choice: expensive evidence requires a payer, and the payer influences which evidence can remain free. RTINGS membership revision

Transferable rule: define a versioned category method before publishing rankings. Store the measurements separately from the verdict. A new product should add to a comparison table rather than trigger an unrelated article.

2. PCPartPicker: aggregate the project, not the parts#

PCPartPicker provides component selection, compatibility guidance, current multi-retailer pricing, price history, alerts, complete builds and community discussion. The user leaves with a build, not a processor page. PCPartPicker About

Its secret is the dependency graph:

chosen use and budget
→ compatible component set
→ warnings and estimated requirements
→ complete bill of materials
→ retailer-level purchase plan

The project becomes saved buyer state. It can be revised when a price changes, a component becomes unavailable or another builder suggests a better arrangement. Affiliate links monetise the decision, but the bill of materials and compatibility rules cause the user to return.

This is stronger than a “best CPU” article for three reasons:

  1. suitability depends on the other selected components;
  2. the customer has a reason to save and share the result;
  3. every product identity can participate in many valid builds.

Transferable rule: the garden equivalent is a saved property and project. A robot mower should be selected against lawn area, zones, slope, boundary system, passages, obstructions, connectivity, weather exposure, service coverage and installation preference. The output should include required accessories and installation steps, not a single ranked product.

3. BrickLink: catalogue, creation and transaction reinforce one another#

BrickLink describes more than 10,000 sellers, more than one million registered members and a catalogue containing over 127,000 unique items. The live catalogue joins sets, parts, minifigures and inventories; its price guide uses actual orders from the prior six months and current listed offers. Studio lets a person design digitally and connect that design to the parts required to build it. BrickLink About, catalogue, price guide

Its flywheel is unusually coherent:

community resolves identity and relationships
→ creators design with that catalogue
→ designs produce exact wanted lists
→ sellers fulfil those lists
→ transactions create price history
→ collectors and creators improve the catalogue

The operator does not have to invent all catalogue labour centrally because contributors receive direct utility: collection records, exact searches, wanted lists, price information and marketplace liquidity. Community is not an audience bolted onto commerce. It helps maintain the substrate of commerce.

Transferable rule: ask experts to contribute only where the contribution improves their own project or professional record. An installer who corrects a product's electrical requirement could receive a reusable specification sheet or qualified-project eligibility. Do not request unpaid moderation for the abstract benefit of the platform.

4. idealo: history and alerts turn price into a product#

idealo claims roughly 50,000 shops and more than 350 million offers on its company page, while its consumer alert page refers to more than 500 million offers. The mismatch itself shows why counts should be time-stamped and treated as operator claims rather than a fixed fact. The stable mechanism is more important: offer comparison is combined with twelve-month price history, favourites and target-price alerts. idealo About Us, price alerts

A current price is easy to copy. A trustworthy time series is not. It must resolve product identity, preserve observations, handle promotions and variants, record shipping and detect stale or implausible values. Alerts then convert that history into retained intent.

idealo's legal notice says it updates data several times daily or in real time when supported, provides a time of last update and asks the buyer to verify final details at the shop. That is a better product promise than “live prices” without an evidence trail. idealo offer-data notice

Transferable rule: display observed_at, source, included delivery and confidence for every offer. Store append-only observations. Let the buyer set a threshold for a complete project, not just an SKU.

5. Chrono24 and Cardmarket: trust is a costed operating layer#

Chrono24 aggregates high-value watches across more than 150 claimed countries. Its buyer proposition includes dealer verification, escrow, proof of ownership, an authenticity commitment and a fourteen-day money-back route. Chrono24 About Us

Cardmarket applies a lighter version to trading cards. Sellers must attach an offer to the correct product page and avoid counterfeits. Its trustee service holds eligible buyer payment until tracked receipt. Cardmarket terms, trustee service

These controls do two economic jobs:

  • make distant or unknown supply acceptable to buyers;
  • create a reason to transact through the platform rather than route around it.

The cost is equally real: identity checks, payment handling, counterfeit expertise, disputes, support and potential loss. A startup cannot claim the trust halo without operating the protection.

Transferable rule: for installed garden products, trust begins with narrower controls: company identity, territory, manufacturer accreditation where applicable, insurance evidence, response time, accepted project type and outcome feedback. Do not call a directory “verified” unless the checks and expiry dates are defined.

6. Auto Trader and Machinio: supply workflow is the deeper subscription moat#

Auto Trader's reporting shows the endpoint of a long marketplace flywheel. It reported 13,942 average retailer forecourts and £2,995 average monthly revenue per retailer for FY2026. It also reports technology and data usage, Deal Builder customers and services for manufacturers and retailers. Auto Trader annual report 2026, FY2026 results

Machinio is a smaller and more directly relevant analogy for machinery. It combines global used-equipment search with software for dealers, and Liquidity Services acquired it in 2018 to add listing and asset-data capabilities to a wider business-surplus marketplace. Machinio About, Liquidity Services history

The compound loop is:

valuable buyer demand
→ suppliers pay and structure inventory
→ workflow tools improve inventory and response
→ buyers receive fresher supply
→ supplier participation becomes more valuable

The order is not reversible. Without buyers, supplier software is another CRM. Without supplier workflow, the marketplace eventually fights stale inventory and slow responses.

Transferable rule: earn the right to sell installer or dealer software. Begin with qualification and outcome records. Productise the repeated pain—specification intake, quote completeness, territory, follow-up or inventory normalisation—after suppliers already value the enquiries.

7. Which and Consumer Reports: rigorous scope is a strategic advantage#

Which says it tests almost 4,000 products in more than 100 categories each year. It also explains that categories are stopped when interest falls or products become obsolete. Its 2025 annual report recorded 500,063 memberships at June 2025, down 4.1%, and 3,390 tested products during the year. Which test selection, Which 2025 annual report

Consumer Reports says it expects to spend more than $30 million testing and rating over 10,000 products and services. Consumer Reports testing

The common lesson is not “spend millions”. It is that credible testing has an opportunity cost. Every added category creates acquisition, storage, method, expertise, update and legal obligations. Stopping a low-value programme is part of the evidence system, not a failure of ambition.

Transferable rule: maintain a test-capital ledger. A proposed test should state the buying decision it changes, expected usable lifetime, retest trigger, products covered and funding source. Reject tests that merely create content theatre.

8. Future: distribution resilience has to be designed#

Future is useful because it shows both the power and pressure of scaled specialist publishing. Its 2025 report describes advertising, affiliate ecommerce, subscriptions, magazines, lead generation, events and services. Its 2025 initiatives included Signal curated commerce collections, Future+ direct membership and Collab creator distribution. The company called Future+ its “Google-Zero” strategy and reported 67,000 new members whose sessions were at least four times longer than unknown users. Those are early company-reported metrics, not an independently verified retention result. Future FY2025 results

In HY2026, Future reported revenue down 8% and said only 16% of total revenue was then directly correlated with website sessions while high-yield direct advertising grew. Future HY2026 results

The important point is not whether one half-year is good or bad. It is that a mature publisher is explicitly building social, email, member, creator, direct-advertising and AI-facing routes because anonymous website sessions are not a sufficient foundation.

Transferable rule: capture consented identity when the user receives an obvious benefit—saved property, price threshold, maintenance schedule, project export or warranty record. A newsletter prompt without retained product utility is not a direct-audience strategy.

How can the mechanisms be scored?#

A proposed feature can be tested against eight questions.

Question Weak answer Strong answer
What fact becomes proprietary? generated summary measured result or retained event
What decision changes? page is easier to read unsuitable products are excluded or project changes
Why will the user return? more articles saved project, alert, collection, workflow or update
Who maintains the record? anonymous automation named process with sources and correction route
Who pays? any advertiser defined merchant, member, supplier or licensee
What can go wrong? low traffic named identity, trust, test, attribution or support failure
Can it travel beyond the website? no structured export, email, API or agent-readable evidence
Does the next user improve it? more page views more history, validated outcomes, supply or corrections

A high score does not guarantee demand. It prevents the company from confusing output volume with accumulated advantage.

What is the recommended mechanism stack?#

The premium-garden business should build four layers in order.

Layer 1: canonical product and offer history#

Resolve manufacturer, model, variant, generation, included equipment and installation path. Preserve every price, stock and warranty observation with timestamp and source. Do not describe an offer as current beyond its freshness window.

Layer 2: one versioned field-test system#

Begin with robotic mowers because the category has a compact high-value product set, a live affiliate route and consequential differences in boundary handling, slope, passage navigation, recovery, setup and service. Publish the protocol before the league table.

Layer 3: saved property and project compatibility#

Turn lawn area, zones, slope, access, obstructions, connectivity and installation preference into explicit constraints. Produce a shortlist, excluded products with reasons, required accessories and a survey or install route.

Layer 4: qualified supplier workflow#

Send an installer or retailer a structured brief rather than a name and phone number. Record acceptance, response, survey, quote, sale, install and later outcome. Only after repeated use should the workflow become a supplier subscription or software product.

This stack combines RTINGS-like evidence, PCPartPicker-like compatibility, idealo-like history and an Auto Trader-like path toward supplier workflow without assuming the scale of any of them.

What remains unproven?#

The cases establish that the mechanisms exist and that established operators invest in them. They do not prove that UK garden buyers will save projects, pay for tests or accept the proposed property model. They do not reveal whether merchants will provide usable feeds, whether installers will respond to structured leads, or whether affiliate revenue can fund field testing.

Those are the next experiments. The secret sauce is a design hypothesis until buyers and suppliers use it repeatedly.
