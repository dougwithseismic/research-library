Shopping Agents, Generated Content and the Decision Web

How merchant-direct feeds, conversational comparison, generated content and attribution pressure change the product-aggregation thesis.

Geography
United Kingdom
Research date
2026-09-03
Status
Published research

The revised AI verdict#

AI makes the original recommendation narrower and stronger. A feed-only comparison site is not merely undifferentiated; it is increasingly a temporary interface over data that merchants can send directly to search and shopping agents. A defensible operator must create evidence, compatibility logic, project context or services that do not already exist in the feed.

The recommendation remains build premium garden and outdoor systems, but only as a project decision company. Do not build a business whose main asset is generic buying guides, rewritten merchant descriptions, a sortable price table or affiliate links. Those layers can be generated, reproduced or bypassed at low marginal cost.

Two separate AI disruptions#

“AI commerce” combines two mechanisms that should not be confused.

Shopping agents compress the buyer journey#

OpenAI now describes product discovery in ChatGPT as a conversational flow that can interpret budget and constraints, compare products side by side, and use merchant feeds supplied directly or through platforms. Google has launched the Universal Commerce Protocol and announced agentic checkout for eligible US product listings, while retaining the retailer as seller of record. These are platform announcements rather than UK adoption or conversion evidence, but they establish technical and strategic direction. OpenAI product discovery Google agentic commerce

An agent can potentially remove several steps that a conventional aggregator monetises:

search → buying guide → category page → comparison table → affiliate click → retailer

becomes:

stated constraints → agent comparison from merchant feeds → retailer checkout

This does not prove that every UK buyer will adopt the shorter path, that agents will always identify the right product, or that affiliate attribution disappears. It does mean the operator should not capitalise a long-term plan on owning the tab-opening and link-clicking steps.

Generated content expands supply#

The second mechanism is a supply shock. Product descriptions, category introductions, “best” lists, comparison summaries, illustrations and promotional variants can now be produced cheaply by merchants, publishers and competitors. The average informational page becomes less scarce before an agent removes any click.

Google's current guidance does not prohibit content because AI helped produce it. It warns that generating many pages without adding user value may violate its scaled-content-abuse policy, and it asks publishers to focus on accuracy, quality, relevance and useful creation context. For ecommerce, it also says AI-generated product titles and descriptions must be separately labelled in Merchant Center and AI-generated imagery must carry specified metadata. Google generative-content guidance

The economic conclusion is more important than the production rule: if a page can be made from the same public specifications and feed fields in an afternoon, it cannot be the moat.

What agents can reproduce#

The following layers have high substitution or compression risk:

  • generic product definitions and basic specification summaries;
  • category pages assembled from merchant-feed descriptions;
  • simple price sorting where the same live offers reach the agent;
  • undifferentiated “best product” lists without disclosed tests or decision rules;
  • first-draft email, social, image and comparison copy;
  • conversational filtering over complete, current and correctly structured catalogue data.

This is especially dangerous for affiliate economics. The traditional publisher is paid because it influences or refers a transaction. If the agent owns discovery and the merchant owns the feed and checkout, the independent publisher may be absent from the measurable path. No public source reviewed for this chapter establishes a settled UK compensation standard for that missing contribution.

What agents still need someone to create#

An agent can reason over a compatibility graph, but it cannot recover a graph that nobody has built. It can summarise a field test, but it cannot truthfully conduct the test. It can compare local installation offers only when current coverage, constraints, capacity and price are available.

The durable assets are therefore upstream of the generated answer:

  1. Original measurements and tests. Noise, practical coverage, setup time, energy use, weather behaviour and failure observations collected under a published method.
  2. A compatibility and constraint graph. Which mower works with which boundary system; which heater, base, supply, ventilation or access conditions make an installation viable; which accessories and replacement parts actually fit.
  3. Project calculators. Structured inputs for space, slope, access, power, planning constraints, service area and budget, with visible rules and uncertainty.
  4. Verified offer history. Product identity, retailer, price, stock, delivery, warranty and observation time, including corrections rather than silent overwrites.
  5. Installation and service capacity. Current postcode coverage, lead time, qualification, inspection, survey and fulfilment routes.
  6. Outcome evidence. Returns, common incompatibilities, installation exceptions, maintenance burden and buyer satisfaction gathered with consent and appropriate aggregation.
  7. Merchant relationships and commercial rights. Reliable feeds, approved media, attribution terms, exclusive packages and permission to use non-public data.

These assets are useful through a website, an API or an agent. The public page becomes one distribution surface rather than the full product.

The right use of generated content#

Use AI where the output can be checked against retained facts:

  • map inconsistent merchant attributes into a proposed canonical schema;
  • identify missing fields, conflicts, stale offers and suspicious price movements;
  • draft comparison structures from verified attributes;
  • produce accessibility variants, translations and internal research summaries;
  • help a buyer turn free text into explicit project constraints;
  • generate follow-up questions when the available facts are insufficient.

Do not use it to:

  • invent a test, ownership experience, review, quote or installer visit;
  • create synthetic “customer” photographs or imply that generated scenes are real installations;
  • fill missing specifications with plausible values;
  • publish hundreds of near-identical intent pages;
  • disguise merchant copy as independent analysis;
  • make a safety, planning, electrical or structural decision outside the operator's competence.

Every generated claim should resolve to a source field, a method, a named assumption or an explicit unknown. Human review is not a magic label: the reviewer needs the evidence and competence to detect an error.

The operating model after agents#

The data pipeline should separate four things:

merchant assertion
  → normalised observation
    → independently verified evidence
      → recommendation or transaction rule

An AI system may assist between stages, but it must not collapse them. A feed saying that a mower covers 3,000 square metres is a merchant assertion. A retained feed snapshot is an observation. A measured test under stated conditions is independent evidence. A recommendation for a particular garden is a rule applied to the buyer's constraints.

This distinction also creates a multi-interface strategy:

  • publish useful human-readable project pages;
  • expose stable structured product and evidence fields for machine consumption;
  • retain first-party tools and saved projects that justify a direct relationship;
  • send the transaction to the retailer or installer under explicit commercial terms;
  • measure assisted discovery, direct return, lead acceptance and completed outcomes rather than affiliate clicks alone.

Do not optimise solely for being quoted by an answer engine. A citation without a visit or attributed outcome may have brand value, but its economics are unknown until observed.

Three scenarios#

Limited adoption#

AI reduces editorial and catalogue labour while most buyers still browse sites and complete retailer checkout. Every competitor receives similar production savings, so the advantage comes from faster verification and better tools, not cheaper prose. The operator should use AI internally and keep testing whether gross contribution per maintained project page improves.

Agent-mediated shopping#

Agents own a material share of discovery and receive current merchant feeds. Generic affiliate pages lose visits and referral credit. The operator distributes its original evidence and compatibility data across interfaces, charges for qualified installed-project demand, and seeks data, tenancy or licensing agreements that do not require a final-click cookie.

Incumbent response#

Retailers, networks, search engines and large publishers combine feeds, transaction history, brand trust and their own assistants. A small aggregator cannot beat their catalogue breadth. It can still win one narrow project by producing better field evidence, local installation intelligence and owner workflows than a horizontal platform can justify collecting.

Kill conditions and the next test#

Reject or redesign the thesis if any of these becomes true:

  • merchants can expose complete project-level compatibility and local installation data directly to major agents;
  • the operator cannot obtain permissioned, reliable product and offer data;
  • original tests and tools do not create measurable assisted actions, saved projects, qualified leads or return visits;
  • agent-originated buyers cannot be attributed or monetised under any viable merchant or lead agreement;
  • maintaining verified evidence costs more than the gross contribution it influences.

The next reversible test is not fifty generated guides. Build one project flow—for example, selecting a robotic mower for a constrained garden—with twelve to twenty canonical products, explicit compatibility rules, retained offer history and at least three original measurements. Present the same evidence through a conventional comparison page and a structured conversational interface. Measure completed specifications, shortlist saves, merchant actions, accepted installer leads and evidence corrections. The human seam remains merchant and buyer interviews plus real conversion cohorts; this desk research does not substitute for them.

Decision#

Proceed with the premium-garden thesis only if the company is willing to own decision evidence, not merely product content. Shopping agents are a distribution threat to the classic affiliate journey and a potential consumer of better structured evidence. Generated content is useful operating leverage and a severe supply-side threat. Neither becomes a moat by itself.
