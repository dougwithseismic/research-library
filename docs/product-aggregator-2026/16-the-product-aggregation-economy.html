The Product-Aggregation Economy

The real value chain across price engines, test publishers, compatibility tools, marketplaces, supplier software and shopping agents.

Geography
United Kingdom
Research date
2026-09-03
Status
Published research

What is the direct answer?#

Product aggregation is not one business model. It is a value chain that begins with a confused buyer and ends with a suitable product, supplier or project. Price-comparison engines, test publishers, compatibility tools, vertical marketplaces, dealer software and shopping agents occupy different positions in that chain.

The strongest companies do not merely collect more products. They own one or more difficult transformations:

merchant records → canonical product identity
product claims → measured evidence
buyer language → explicit constraints
separate components → compatible project
volatile offers → trustworthy history
unknown seller → acceptable transaction risk
fragmented supply → reliable availability
one visit → saved intent and repeat use

A catalogue is an input. The business begins when an operator makes one of those transformations reliable enough that a buyer, merchant, dealer or another platform will pay for it.

Why does this economy look shallower than it is?#

From the outside, many product businesses look like pages with pictures, specifications, prices and links. That interface hides very different operating systems.

idealo has to match hundreds of millions of offers, update them frequently and preserve an organic ordering that it says is independent of merchant payment. RTINGS buys products, retains them, publishes versioned test benches and reruns tests. PCPartPicker maintains component compatibility and turns a basket into a bill of materials. Chrono24 operates dealer verification, proof-of-ownership, escrow and authentication controls. Auto Trader sells data and workflow into the suppliers that provide its inventory.

Those companies can all render a product card. The product card is not the shared moat.

The shallow interpretation of aggregation is:

more feeds → more pages → more search traffic → more affiliate clicks

The deeper economy is:

better identity or evidence → lower decision risk → retained buyer intent
→ measurable supplier value → recurring commercial relationship

That second chain is harder to establish, but it explains why some operators survive changes in search, ownership and interface while thin publishers disappear.

Several parties may touch the same order, but they cannot all claim its value. A publisher can influence a sale without receiving last-click commission. A dealer marketplace can generate an enquiry without owning the eventual transaction. A shopping agent can synthesize the choice without originating the underlying measurement. An evidence-led plan has to name which unit is actually sold and which participant is expected to pay.

What are the main archetypes?#

1. Horizontal price engines#

idealo, PriceRunner, PriceSpy and Kelkoo began with broad comparison but now expose different strategic directions.

idealo combines offer breadth with price history, alerts and timestamps. PriceRunner combines price comparison, product reviews, testing, payment and delivery information and now sits inside Klarna. Kelkoo describes merchant performance products and a publisher network with link, search and feed integrations. The common operational work is matching, freshness and measurable merchant traffic; the consumer brand is only one distribution surface. idealo About Us, Klarna 2022 annual report, Kelkoo Group

Their advantage is scale. Their weakness is that price aggregation is closest to the search platforms and shopping agents able to substitute it.

2. Test-led decision publishers#

Which, Consumer Reports, RTINGS, OutdoorGearLab, Expert Reviews and Future's specialist brands add observation rather than only description.

The capital models differ sharply. Consumer Reports says it expects to spend more than $30 million testing and rating over 10,000 products and services. RTINGS combines membership, affiliate, advertising and content licensing. Which combines membership, affiliate commission and endorsement licensing while buying nearly all tested products. OutdoorGearLab says it is affiliate-funded, refuses ads and free test units and purchases the products it reviews. Consumer Reports testing, RTINGS About Us, Which independence, OutdoorGearLab methodology

These are not content factories. They are evidence-production organisations. Their core questions are what to test, how to keep methods comparable, which products to retain, when to retest and how to fund the work without selling the answer.

3. Compatibility and project systems#

PCPartPicker is the clearest expression of a project aggregator. It does not ask the buyer to choose a processor, motherboard, memory and power supply independently. It assembles a complete build, filters known incompatibilities, warns about conflicts, tracks current retailer prices and preserves a shareable bill of materials. PCPartPicker About

BrickLink has a related structure in a different category. Its catalogue connects sets, parts, minifigures and inventories; price guidance draws on actual orders and current offers; Studio lets a person design before creating a wanted list and sourcing the parts. The design and the product graph create demand for each other. BrickLink About, catalogue, price guide

The scarce asset is not a review. It is the rule system that turns components into a valid outcome.

4. Specialist marketplaces#

Reverb, Chrono24, Discogs and Cardmarket aggregate heterogeneous inventory whose exact identity or counterparty matters.

Discogs relies on contributors to distinguish physical releases before sellers list against them. Cardmarket requires listings to attach to the correct card page and uses a trustee service for qualifying trades. Chrono24 adds dealer verification, escrow, proof of ownership and authentication. Reverb concentrates instrument supply and the vocabulary buyers use to distinguish it. Discogs database architecture, Cardmarket trustee service, Chrono24 About Us, Reverb billing policy

These businesses win when they reduce identity and transaction risk enough to expand the supply a buyer will consider. A generic product feed cannot do that for a used guitar, watch, record pressing or trading card.

5. Dealer search and supplier operating systems#

Auto Trader, CarGurus, Machinio and TractorHouse monetise suppliers as well as buyer discovery.

Auto Trader's FY2026 results reported £2,995 average monthly revenue per retailer across 13,942 average retailer forecourts. Its reporting describes data, APIs and Deal Builder beside marketplace access. Machinio combines machinery search with dealer software. TractorHouse sells dealer inventory, digital and print exposure, websites, ecommerce and auction products while giving buyers saved searches, alerts and detailed equipment filters. Auto Trader FY2026 results, Machinio, TractorHouse advertising

The sequence matters. Supplier software is powerful once buyer demand makes participation valuable. Selling an empty-marketplace CRM first does not recreate Auto Trader.

6. Retailer-owned decision systems#

Specialist retailers are not neutral aggregators, but they compete for the same customer decision. Home Leisure Direct can combine advice, showroom, product, delivery and installation. Kiln Crafts publishes electrical, placement and ventilation guidance and sells complete ceramics-studio bundles. The Pottery People publishes comparison guides over its own range. Games Room Company combines unusual stock, restoration expertise and service.

The retailer has a structural advantage: it captures product margin and can answer questions close to fulfilment. The independent aggregator has to create cross-merchant truth, deeper evidence, better compatibility or more valuable project context. Otherwise the buyer can receive adequate advice directly from the party that holds the stock.

7. Machine-facing aggregation#

Google and OpenAI now accept direct merchant product data and can interpret a buyer's requirements conversationally. Google's Universal Commerce Protocol announcement describes agentic discovery and checkout routes; OpenAI describes merchant feeds and retailer integrations for product discovery. Neither source proves UK adoption, publisher compensation or conversion, but both establish that structured merchant data can bypass the old page sequence. Google agentic commerce, OpenAI product discovery

This does not eliminate the economy. It changes who renders the answer. A versioned lawn-mower test, a compatibility rule, verified price history or current installer record can still have value when an agent is the interface. A rewritten merchant description cannot.

Where are the durable value pools?#

The mechanism ledger points to seven value pools.

  1. Canonical identity. The operator knows that two merchant rows are the same item—or that two visually similar collector items are not.
  2. Proprietary observation. The operator has measured performance, retained a historical offer, inspected a condition or observed an outcome.
  3. Decision logic. The operator can translate constraints into a defensible shortlist or complete bill of materials.
  4. Trust operations. The operator verifies a dealer, holds payment, authenticates an item or enforces a meaningful mark.
  5. Retained intent. The buyer saves a project, follows a price, owns a collection, maintains a build or receives a useful alert.
  6. Supplier workflow. Inventory, quote, project or dealer tools create recurring commercial dependence and improve the marketplace input.
  7. Distribution resilience. Direct membership, email, apps, community, APIs, partners and machine-readable evidence reduce reliance on a single search surface.

Price and affiliate links can monetise these assets. They rarely create them.

What does a useful flywheel look like?#

The best flywheels reward each participant for improving the system.

buyer saves constraints
    ↓
better structured demand reaches suppliers
    ↓
suppliers return cleaner availability and outcome data
    ↓
tests and rules become more accurate
    ↓
recommendations become more trustworthy
    ↓
more buyers save projects and return

PCPartPicker adds a second loop: shared builds make the compatibility system easier to understand, which produces more builds. BrickLink adds a creation loop: Studio designs create wanted lists, transactions create price history and catalogue corrections improve future designs. Auto Trader adds a supply loop: retailer software improves the stock and journey that attract the next buyer.

A feed-only site has no equivalent loop. More rows create more maintenance and more indexable pages, not necessarily more value.

The sequence prevents an important error. An operator cannot sell valuable insight before it has accumulated decision data, supplier software before it has supplier demand, or tenancy before it has an audience worth renting.

What should a founder take from the economy?#

Do not start by choosing a page type. Choose one expensive decision whose structure is currently missing.

Then name:

  • the buyer state you will retain;
  • the evidence you will create rather than import;
  • the rule or workflow that improves with use;
  • the commercial event a supplier can verify;
  • the interface-independent record that remains valuable if search traffic changes.

For premium garden systems, that means a saved property and project, category-specific compatibility, real observed prices, bounded field tests and a qualified installation route. The website can publish buying guides, but the underlying product is a garden-project decision graph.

What remains unknown?#

The operator evidence shows mechanisms and current products. It does not establish a market-wide success rate.

Private operator revenue, profit, retention, category contribution and traffic concentration are mostly unknown. First-party scale claims are not audited. A current product page proves that the offer exists, not that customers use it enough to produce attractive economics. Acquisitions establish strategic willingness to pay at one date, not the acquired company's current value. Court damages establish a finding at one stage of litigation, not collected cash.

Those boundaries are retained in the operator census, failure-case ledger and capital and ownership ledger.

This publication distinguishes observed evidence, derived calculations, commercial inference and unknowns.
