The decision#

Build a transparent local demand and qualification company, not a portfolio of fake local contractors.

The deeper pass changed the decision. The first buyer-discovery wave is asbestos surveys in Manchester, fire risk assessments in Manchester, drain unblocking in Bristol and boiler replacement in Edinburgh. Start with one partner-led validation lane, not four simultaneous sites. Use accepted-call, accepted-lead or qualified-appointment pricing and move to a base-plus-performance contract only after at least 90 days of buyer and cohort evidence.

Commercial cleaning in Edinburgh is paused. Its original headline row is dominated by one exceptional month, while the generic local-geo basket is only 90 searches and reports no average CPC. The supplier market exists; the search-demand thesis needs independent verification.

Do not create speculative Google Business Profiles. Google explicitly lists lead-generation companies as ineligible. Any eligible local profile must belong to a genuine business that meets customers in person, with the business owner controlling its authoritative website and phone details.

The headline evidence#

  • The broad screen contains 910 explicit city-service phrases: 35 niches × 26 UK cities.
  • It separately contains 70 national-intent phrases: one generic and one near me form per niche.
  • The cross-check adds 144 generic-service rows inside nine city geo targets, plus expanded explicit and local-geo variants. A further read-only pass adds 99 service-cluster rows, 33 buyer-intent rows and 17 platform-brand rows.
  • In the comparable 16-service local-geo basket, London leads at 23,510 searches and £13.47 weighted CPC; Bristol is the most CPC-dense substantial non-London basket at 1,250 and £12.01.
  • Manchester asbestos returned a final expanded basket of 290 searches at £19.50 explicit weighted CPC and 290 at £13.31 local-geo weighted CPC.
  • Manchester fire-risk assessment returned 550 searches at £26.02 explicit and 280 at £9.10 local-geo weighted CPC.
  • Bristol drainage returned a 690-search local-geo keyword basket at £6.66 weighted CPC and the strongest fast-cycle validation profile.
  • Edinburgh boiler installation returned £19.39 explicit and £13.67 local-geo weighted CPC across final expanded baskets.
  • Edinburgh cleaning's expanded explicit basket peaks at 44,440 in April 2026, 7.87 times its monthly mean; the local-geo basket is only 90 with no reported average CPC.
  • The operator atlas covers 32 UK-relevant products across search, directories, marketplaces, direct lead supply, agencies, trust schemes and routing infrastructure.
  • Companies House accounts now compare seven selected operators. Five disclose latest-period turnover totalling £298.8 million, but the products, geographies and periods differ, so this is evidence of scale rather than a sector-total estimate.
  • MyBuilder's latest filed operating margin was about 22.7%; Bark reported an operating loss; Yell's platform and digital-marketing-services lines declined while its Solutions line grew. Platform revenue is real, but profitability and revenue mix vary sharply.
  • Buyer-side Google Ads observations include trade leads at £35.15 CPC, roofing leads at £29.45, boiler leads at £22.48 and SEO for plumbers at £16.85. These show paid commercial attention, not a market lead tariff.
  • London led the consistent city basket with 40,970 searches at £16.77 weighted CPC, but it is an expansion market rather than the recommended learning market.
  • The most detailed public UK success case found is anonymous and self-reported: £83,000 build cost and £4,000 monthly rent.
  • A documented UK boiler-site failure found both slow search progress and no responses from prospective tenants.
  • Google AI Mode has been available in the UK since July 2025. Available research suggests generic and hybrid information queries face much more AI-summary exposure than direct local action queries.
  • Generated content separately increases the supply of commodity service and location pages. The recommendation therefore excludes page-factory economics and treats verified provider, qualification and outcome data as the potential asset.

Evidence classes#

  • Observed: returned by Google Ads or stated on a live source.
  • Derived: arithmetic from observed or explicitly assumed inputs.
  • Inference: a commercial judgement supported by evidence but not proven by it.
  • Unknown: something that needs buyer, traffic or operational validation.

Average monthly search volume is historical and rounded. CPC is an advertising-auction signal, not organic difficulty, lead price or profit. Explicit city phrases under the UK target are not the same as generic phrases measured inside a city geo target. National, near-me and city datasets overlap and must not be added. Companies House filings are company-supplied registry evidence and do not prove trading quality or willingness to buy leads.
