How the score works#

The downloadable scorecard assigns ordinal 1–5 judgements across demand, CPC signal, buyer value, organic fit, platform safety, operating simplicity, durability and fit with a lead or tenancy model. A total out of 40 aids comparison; it is not a probability of success.

Raw demand does not automatically win. A candidate can score highly as a search business and poorly as classic rank-and-rent. Self storage is the clearest example.

The deeper pass added a non-scored gate: explicit-city and local-geo evidence agreement. A high ordinal total cannot override a major query anomaly. This moved Edinburgh cleaning out of band 1 and Manchester asbestos into the first buyer-discovery wave.

Paused despite the original headline#

Commercial cleaning — Edinburgh#

Why it is paused: the original phrase reports 2,900 searches at £14.32 CPC, but April 2026 alone reports 22,200. The expanded explicit basket peaks at 44,440, 7.87 times its monthly mean. The generic basket measured inside Edinburgh totals only 90 and reports no average CPC.

Commercial product if verified: a premises survey request with sector, area, frequency, access, current contract, specialist requirements and decision date, sold as a qualified appointment.

Restart condition: a fresh historical read, real impression data or Search Console evidence explains the spike and establishes useful commercial query volume.

Priority band 1#

Asbestos surveys — Manchester#

Why it survives: strongest explicit/local CPC agreement in the priority screen: £20.95 and £16.23 weighted CPC respectively. Compliance intent and provider verification can create transaction value.

Product: premises and survey-type triage, appropriately competent or accredited provider verification, quote request and report-turnaround comparison.

Commercial model: verified-provider appointment. Domestic survey values may cap lead economics, so sell only after contribution is known.

Stop condition: no appropriately qualified partners, survey and removal incentives cannot be kept clear, or buyer contribution cannot support the acquisition hurdle.

Fire risk assessment — Manchester#

Why it survives: 260 searches at £27.11 CPC; clear duty and recurring review need; valuable business and landlord audience.

Product: premises-specific triage, competent-assessor verification, quote collection and report-turnaround comparison.

Commercial model: qualified appointment or territory hybrid.

Stop condition: buyers reject small-premises leads, cannot evidence competence for the premises types marketed, or institutional results leave too little commercial click opportunity.

Boiler installation — Edinburgh#

Why it survives: 880 searches at £16.04; roughly £3,000 average gas-combi supply-and-install value in Checkatrade's 2026 guide; seasonal but substantial project economics.

Product: boiler and property specification followed by a home survey.

Commercial model: accepted lead, later hybrid or tenant floor.

Stop condition: no two Gas Safe partners with capacity, acquisition economics require misleading finance claims, or repair intent overwhelms installation intent.

Drain unblocking — Bristol#

Why it survives: 590 searches at £16.09; urgent intent; phone qualification is measurable.

Product: one-tap call with private/shared-drain, symptoms, location and dispatch triage.

Commercial model: connected qualified call with duplicate, duration, geography and spam rules.

Stop condition: buyers miss calls, arrival coverage is fictional, or simple-job margin cannot support the call price.

Priority band 2#

Garage-door repair — Nottingham#

A lean specialist test. Compete through photo diagnosis, clear price structure and same-day availability rather than content volume. Local incumbents are real but the page footprint required is modest.

House clearance — Newcastle#

Strong local phrase demand and a fragmented provider market. Verification of waste-carrier status and clear disposal practice can create trust. Photos and item classes materially improve quote quality.

Loft conversions — Bristol#

The £24.06 CPC is compelling and the job is valuable, but conversion and attribution are slow. Sell a qualified design conversation, not a raw name and phone number. Expect planning and property constraints.

Resin driveways — Southampton#

High CPC, high project value and relatively low volume. Treat as a lumpy booked-survey business with long lookback windows. Do not promise a fixed lead floor before a seasonal cohort exists.

Spray-foam removal — regional UK#

The national phrase produces 1,600 searches at £26.06, while city phrases are sparse. That is a specialist-content and regional-routing opportunity. It also may be a transient wave tied to mortgage and survey concerns, so durability receives a lower score.

Priority band 3#

Emergency plumbing in Reading and similar cities has valuable intent but brutal operations. A missed midnight call is not an SEO problem; it is a broken product. Enter only with a live buyer and dispatch standard.

Tree surgery in Bristol has useful volume and job value but lower CPC. Photo-based lead qualification and permission or access questions can add value, though seasonality matters.

Skip hire in Birmingham has exceptional volume but a £2.42 city CPC. Booking, size selection, permit information and availability are better value propositions than renting generic calls.

Explicit rejections for launch#

Regulated finance and legal#

Mortgage broker and personal injury keywords show high auction value. That is not permission to publish. FCA authorisation, financial promotions, introducer controls and claims-management rules make them poor first verticals. The buyer and compliance system should be designed with specialists before any acquisition.

Health#

Dental implants and private ADHD assessment combine high value with clinical claims, sensitive data and provider-quality consequences. The research does not justify entering them as a generic lead publisher.

Funeral services#

Funeral searches are commercially meaningful but concern vulnerable consumers and urgent, sensitive decisions. The reputational and advertising burden is disproportionate for an early test.

Emergency locksmith#

The CPC is extraordinary, but so are fraud, trust, price-abuse and map-pack risks. It belongs with an operator that can prove genuine local coverage, pricing standards and call handling—not a speculative publisher learning the model.

Portfolio rule#

Do not build all candidates. Validate two contrasting markets, keep one operational comparison, and maintain a rejection log. A credible first year is six to twelve territories inside one or two verticals, with shared qualification and buyer operations. Thirty-five unrelated micro-sites would multiply every weakness this report identifies.

This publication distinguishes observed evidence, derived calculations, commercial inference and unknowns.
