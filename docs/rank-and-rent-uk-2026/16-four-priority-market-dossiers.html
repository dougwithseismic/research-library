Four Priority Market Dossiers

Detailed demand, intent, buyer, qualification, economic, content and stop-condition analysis for the four recommended cells.

Geography
United Kingdom
Research date
2026-09-03
Status
Published research

How to read the dossiers#

Each dossier uses the expanded explicit-city basket and the matching generic basket measured inside the city. Close variants were deduplicated within each generated cluster. The two methods are shown separately and must not be added.

The dossier dataset applies a single 7% click-to-accepted-lead scenario to local weighted CPC only to expose paid-validation pressure. It is not a forecast. Public job prices are decision anchors from marketplace guides, not transaction records or contractor margin data.

1. Manchester fire-risk assessment#

Demand and auction#

  • explicit-city cluster: 12 rows, 550 average monthly searches, £26.02 weighted CPC;
  • local-geo cluster: 12 rows, 280 searches, £9.10 weighted CPC;
  • modelled paid cost per accepted lead at 7%: £129.94;
  • public guide: low, middle and high average assessment prices of £324, £424 and £674.50, within a broader £200–£1,200 range.

The explicit and local methods agree on existence and commerciality, while the CPC gap warns against using the explicit basket as a paid budget. The query set includes a mix of generic assessment, commercial and possible landlord/HMO intent. Those are different products.

Customer and buyer#

Possible requesters include responsible persons for workplaces, landlords and managing agents, HMO operators, construction or property professionals and small business owners. The buyer is a competent fire-risk assessor or regional fire-safety firm with Manchester capacity.

GOV.UK says the responsible person must carry out and regularly review a fire risk assessment and may appoint a competent person. Workplace fire safety The publisher must not imply that a generic referral itself satisfies the duty.

Qualification schema#

Capture property use, number of floors, approximate floor area, occupancy, existing assessment date, reason for assessment, fire-alarm context, postcode, access timing and whether the requester is the responsible person or authorised agent. Never ask the visitor to self-certify technical compliance.

Commercial unit#

An exclusive scoped opportunity or attended appointment is more appropriate than a raw form. A provisional interview band of £90–£220 is deliberately unvalidated. At the modelled £130 paid acquisition cost, a low lead fee cannot support search ads. Organic acquisition or a stronger funnel is required.

Defensible content#

Build a Manchester building-type and duty-holder decision tree, a dated price survey, an evidence checklist, assessor credential explanations, turnaround observations and anonymised reasons requests are rejected. This material can remain useful if an AI summary answers the generic definition.

Stop conditions#

Stop if fewer than three capable buyers accept the same lead definition, if buyers cannot evidence competence, if most demand is low-value domestic work outside their desired mix, or if the operator would need to make professional judgments during qualification.

2. Manchester asbestos surveys#

Demand and auction#

  • explicit-city cluster: 11 returned rows, 290 searches, £19.50 weighted CPC;
  • local-geo cluster: 12 rows, 290 searches, £13.31 weighted CPC;
  • modelled paid cost per accepted lead at 7%: £190.16;
  • public guide: domestic management survey average £250, commercial average £400, broad £150–£600 range; refurbishment and demolition survey around £350.

The two location methods agree unusually closely on basket volume. The economic problem is obvious: the modelled paid accepted-lead cost can consume much of a low-ticket survey price. The cell needs a high conversion rate, valuable commercial mix, follow-on value, cheaper organic acquisition or higher-value qualification.

Customer and buyer#

Intent can come from a homeowner planning work, a landlord or dutyholder managing premises, a buyer's solicitor or survey process, or a contractor needing refurbishment evidence. The buyer is a competent survey organisation with suitable accreditation and a defined service radius.

HSE recommends checking competence and notes UKAS accreditation for surveying organisations. HSE asbestos survey guidance The publisher should explain types of survey and route requests; it should not select a survey type as professional advice.

Qualification schema#

Capture property use and approximate construction year, reason for survey, planned intrusive work, domestic or commercial status, dutyholder relationship, address, access, desired report date and whether samples or air testing are already involved. Store only what is necessary and pass it under an explicit privacy notice.

Commercial unit#

Use an exclusive accepted lead with a precise rejection taxonomy. Provisional interview band: £110–£220. A contractor may value the lead above the standalone survey margin if it produces recurring portfolio work or follow-on analytical services, but that must be observed rather than assumed.

Defensible content#

The moat is not hundreds of suburb pages. It is a current local turnaround and pricing dataset, property-context questionnaire, accreditation explainer, redacted example-report anatomy and buyer outcome loop.

Stop conditions#

Stop if buyers rely on ambiguous competence claims, if the majority of forms are tenants without authority, if data cannot distinguish management from refurbishment intent, or if the accepted lead value cannot cover qualification and acquisition.

3. Bristol drainage#

Demand and auction#

  • explicit-city cluster: 13 rows, 1,600 searches, £14.85 weighted CPC;
  • local-geo cluster: 13 rows, 690 searches, £6.66 weighted CPC;
  • modelled paid cost per accepted lead at 7%: £95.12;
  • public guide anchors: £60 standard call-out, £180 emergency call-out, £85 rodding, £185 jetting, £200–£300 CCTV survey and roughly £110 per metre for relining.

This is the largest and least CPC-expensive local basket of the four. The original priority series was also comparatively steady. That makes it the strongest call-funnel test, not necessarily the highest-margin business.

Customer and buyer#

The visitor frequently wants immediate availability, a price boundary and evidence that the company covers the postcode. Buyers range from owner-driver engineers to regional drainage firms. Capacity and answer rate can change within a day.

Qualification schema#

The call path should establish postcode, inside or outside blockage, residential or commercial, flooding or health risk, shared drain possibility, previous attempt, access and urgency. The form path can additionally distinguish jetting, CCTV, repair and planned maintenance.

Commercial unit#

A connected qualified call is cleaner than a generic lead. Test £35–£90 only through buyer interviews and a capped pilot. Define minimum duration, caller geography, service category, existing-customer and duplicate windows, spam, wrong numbers, emergency availability and credit timing.

Because many jobs are low ticket, the buyer cannot fund a lead price derived mechanically from £6.66 CPC. Route density, repeat commercial work and CCTV/repair mix may improve value; travel and missed calls destroy it.

Defensible content#

Useful assets include live coverage, an honest price and method guide, local responsibility guidance for private versus public sewers, Bristol postcode response observations, a symptom-to-service triage tool and first-party resolution data. Avoid fabricated local addresses or a Business Profile owned by the lead generator.

Stop conditions#

Stop if no buyer answers at least 80% of routed calls in agreed hours, if accepted calls close below a buyer-agreed threshold for two cohorts, if call credits exceed 20%, or if a dominant share is outside profitable travel radius.

4. Edinburgh boiler installation#

Demand and auction#

  • explicit-city cluster: 13 rows, 1,920 searches, £19.39 weighted CPC;
  • local-geo cluster: 13 rows, 420 searches, £13.67 weighted CPC;
  • modelled paid cost per accepted lead at 7%: £195.35;
  • public guide: £2,500–£4,000 for a like-for-like combi replacement, £3,000–£15,000 broad total range and £1,000–£2,000 installation component.

The ticket can support an appointment product, but the intent basket will include repair, price research and possibly grants or non-installation questions. Qualification must isolate a quote-ready replacement cohort.

Customer and buyer#

The target visitor is an owner or authorised landlord with a serviceable property, a replacement reason and a realistic timescale. The buyer is a Gas Safe-registered installer or capable regional heating company with survey and installation capacity. Registration must be checked against the authoritative register; the lead site is not the registered provider unless it actually fulfils.

Qualification schema#

Capture ownership or authority, current boiler and fuel, fault or replacement reason, property type, bedrooms/bathrooms, desired timing, postcode, access, finance interest only where appropriately handled, and appointment availability. Do not infer system specification from a marketing form.

Commercial unit#

The best test is an attended quotation, not a phone number. Provisional interview band: £100–£250 per attended quote or a lower base plus a verified won-job component. Cancellation, no-access, tenant-without-authority and existing-customer rules must be explicit.

Defensible content#

Build a local quote preparation tool, transparent installed-price survey, decision support for repair versus replacement, property-input checklist, verified installer availability and anonymised quote-to-install timelines. Generic “best boiler” summaries are the part most exposed to AI substitution.

Stop conditions#

Stop if fewer than three installers will share quote outcomes, if finance or grant claims would require permissions the operator lacks, if buyer capacity is seasonal and unreported, or if attended-appointment contribution cannot support acquisition and cancellations.

Portfolio sequence#

These are research priorities, not four simultaneous launches.

  1. Validate Manchester fire or asbestos through buyer interviews because qualification and professional context create defensibility.
  2. Validate Bristol drainage through a capped call experiment because it provides faster outcome cycles.
  3. Validate Edinburgh boilers only with a buyer capable of reporting attended quotes and installations.
  4. Choose one first build based on buyer evidence, not the highest spreadsheet score.

One market cell should reach a 90-day retained cohort before the second receives material content or link investment.

This publication distinguishes observed evidence, derived calculations, commercial inference and unknowns.
