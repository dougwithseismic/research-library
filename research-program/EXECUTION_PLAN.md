This is the ordered execution plan for the research programme in `program.json`. The machine-readable programme is authoritative for scope, gates, dependencies, artifacts, and human seams.

## Operating rule

Work top-to-bottom within each phase. A paper may progress autonomously through public, registry, read-only demand, modelling, synthesis, and validation work. Interviews, outreach, campaigns, account mutations, purchases, professional sign-off, confidential deal access, and production publication remain human-authorised seams.

AI disruption is not a decorative chapter. Every paper must establish the present value chain before testing substitution, interface change, agent bypass, operating leverage, incumbent response, and the scarce asset that may remain in 2028.
