# Autonomous AI-disruption research plan

This is the ordered execution plan for the research programme in `program.json`. The machine-readable programme is authoritative for scope, gates, dependencies, artifacts, and human seams.

## Status legend

- `[ ]` queued
- `[~]` built to an external evidence seam
- `[x]` decision-grade and validated

## Operating rule

Work top-to-bottom within each phase. A paper may progress autonomously through public, registry, read-only demand, modelling, synthesis, and validation work. Interviews, outreach, campaigns, account mutations, purchases, professional sign-off, confidential deal access, and production publication remain human-authorised seams.

AI disruption is not a decorative chapter. Every paper must establish the present value chain before testing substitution, generated-content supply, interface change, agent bypass, operating leverage, incumbent response, and the scarce asset that may remain in 2028.

## Existing-series evidence seams after the retrofit

These do not block safe desk research on the new programme, but they must remain visible in related conclusions:

- Product aggregation: agent and generated-content mechanisms are now mapped, but no original merchant, publisher, installer, or buyer interviews—or agent-referred conversion cohorts—exist yet.
- Rank-and-rent: query-level AI exposure and generated-content economics are mapped, while buyer interviews, controlled acquisition tests, and first-party generative-search conversion cohorts remain unperformed.
- Events: organiser tooling, content abundance, synthetic companionship, and physical-world durability are mapped; attendee, non-returner, venue, host, and live cohort fieldwork remain unperformed.

Raw interview material, private exports, and large source captures belong in `private-data/`. Only aggregated, redacted, generated material may enter `docs/`, and only through a separately authorised publication step.
