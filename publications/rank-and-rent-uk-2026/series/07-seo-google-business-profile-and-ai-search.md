# SEO, Google Business Profile and AI search

## The material platform finding

Google lists lead-generation agents or companies as ineligible for a Business Profile. A business must generally make in-person contact with customers during stated hours. Only an owner or authorised representative may verify and manage the profile. Google says the profile website and phone should be the business's authoritative contacts and be verifiable by the business owner; the owner must own and manage the website content. [Google profile eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en)

That removes the common tactic of creating a speculative profile for a lead site and later swapping the number or website to a tenant.

## A compliant profile architecture

There are two clean choices.

**Organic-only intermediary:** the publisher makes its lead-generation role clear and ranks normal web pages. It does not pretend to be the service business or claim a profile.

**Tenant-owned local presence:** a genuine provider owns its eligible profile and authoritative local website. The publisher is an authorised marketing and technology partner. The arrangement is commercially closer to performance marketing or licensed infrastructure than the pure digital-landlord story.

For service-area businesses, Google says there should be one profile for the whole area served, the address should be hidden if customers are not served there, and up to 20 specific service areas may be entered. Overall coverage should ordinarily remain within about two hours' driving time. [Google service-area guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en)

Virtual offices, mailboxes and borrowed addresses are not an acceptable growth system.

## The web-spam collision

Google's policies create three direct risks:

1. **Doorway abuse:** multiple sites or city pages built to funnel visitors to one actual destination.
2. **Scaled-content abuse:** large amounts of unoriginal content created mainly to rank, whether generated by AI or people.
3. **Link spam:** buying or selling links for ranking credit and automated link creation.

[Google Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)

A single vertical can legitimately cover many places, but every published page needs a useful local purpose and a browsable hierarchy. If provider coverage, price, response, regulations, evidence and user workflow are identical, the site probably does not need separate pages.

## AI Mode changed the research journey

Google began rolling AI Mode out in Britain on 28 July 2025. It uses query fan-out and is designed for complex multi-part questions and follow-ups. Google says early users asked questions two or three times longer than conventional queries. [Google UK AI Mode announcement](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/around-the-globe/google-europe/united-kingdom/ai-mode-search-uk/)

That matters because rank-and-rent sites historically acquire authority through supporting questions: cost, timing, symptoms, regulations and comparisons. Those are precisely the queries an answer engine can satisfy.

## What the click evidence does and does not say

Pew's US browsing panel found traditional result clicks in 8% of visits with an AI summary versus 15% without one, AI-summary source clicks in 1%, and more session endings where a summary appeared. Long and question-form queries triggered summaries more often. [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)

Ahrefs estimated a 58% lower position-one CTR when an AI Overview was present in its December 2025 comparison. Its 300,000-keyword method focused on informational queries and should not be applied directly to local emergency terms. [Ahrefs study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/)

Whitespark's 540-query local study provides the best intent distinction found:

| Intent        | AI Overview | Local pack |
| ------------- | ----------: | ---------: |
| Direct local  |         15% |        93% |
| Informational |         92% |         6% |
| Hybrid        |         97% |        17% |

The study covered six industries in Houston, Phoenix and Denver, not the UK. It is evidence for a pattern to test, not a British benchmark. [Whitespark study](https://whitespark.ca/blog/case-study-the-prevalence-of-ai-overviews-in-local-search/)

## The resulting content strategy

Protect direct-action demand and make supporting information proprietary.

**Direct action pages** should expose service area, current availability, answer time, provider identity, qualification and booking.

**Hybrid pages** should combine local price or requirement evidence with a next action. Generic prose about national averages will be summarised.

**Informational pages** should exist where the brand has original facts, tools or professional review. Do not publish a question merely because keyword software found it.

Examples of added value:

- recent local quote ranges with methodology and sample size;
- response-time and availability observations;
- verified registration or accreditation status;
- real project photographs and outcomes;
- calculators using category-specific constraints;
- responsibility tools such as private versus shared drainage;
- premises-type qualification for fire assessments;
- report turnaround and sampling scope for asbestos surveys.

## Entity and citation design

AI visibility is partly a verification problem. Keep provider name, company identity, service area, phone, registrations, opening hours and pricing facts consistent across the publisher, provider site, relevant registers and reputable directories.

Do not add `LocalBusiness` structured data that implies the intermediary is the contractor. Structured data must describe the visible page and real entity. Where the page lists providers, model the publisher, service and provider relationships accurately and conservatively.

## AI production policy

Use AI to:

- cluster and classify enquiries;
- draft interview and page structures;
- extract entities from approved sources;
- check duplicates, missing fields and unsupported claims;
- summarise calls under an appropriate privacy workflow;
- propose internal links and maintenance alerts.

Do not use AI to fabricate local experience, credentials, reviews, prices or jobs, or to produce hundreds of city variants. Google's people-first guidance asks who created content, how it was produced and why it exists. [Google people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)

## Measurement in 2026

Google says dedicated Search Console reports for generative-AI features were available to all websites worldwide by 31 August 2026. They expose impressions, pages, countries, devices and time. [Google AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports)

Track four separate cohorts:

- conventional organic direct-action landings;
- conventional organic informational landings;
- generative-AI visibility;
- leads and accepted leads by landing page and intent.

An increase in AI impressions with falling clicks can still be useful only if branded search, direct visits or qualified leads rise. Visibility alone is not the outcome.
