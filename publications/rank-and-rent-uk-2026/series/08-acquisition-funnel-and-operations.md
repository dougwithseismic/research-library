# Acquisition, funnel and lead operations

## Buyer-first acquisition

The first acquisition task is not links or paid search. It is acquiring two credible buyers for a sharply defined job cohort.

For each target market:

1. Build a list of at least 20 providers with visible territory, credentials, reviews, service mix and contact route.
2. Speak with at least ten owner-operators or commercial leads.
3. Secure two written trial agreements using the same accepted-lead definition.
4. Confirm answer time, capacity, service exclusions and weekly caps.
5. Define how outcomes and disputes are returned.

One buyer is not a market. It is concentration risk.

## Buyer interview

Ask for numbers and operational facts:

- Which job types are most profitable after variable costs?
- Which postcodes and travel times work?
- What minimum job value is needed?
- What share of enquiries connect, quote and close?
- What are the top rejection reasons?
- How quickly can calls and forms be answered?
- How much new work can be accepted next week?
- What is currently paid for Checkatrade, Bark, Google Ads or agencies?
- What would make a lead credit automatic?
- Will the buyer share outcome bands under a data-processing agreement?

Avoid asking only “would you pay for more leads?” Almost every business likes the idea; few will commit to the workflow.

## Category qualification schemas

### Commercial cleaning

- property and sector;
- floor area and number of sites;
- cleaning frequency and access window;
- washrooms, kitchens and consumables;
- current provider and contract end;
- TUPE possibility;
- specialist or clinical needs;
- target decision and start dates.

The valuable event is usually a qualified site visit, not a callback.

### Fire risk assessment

- premises use and postcode;
- floor area, storeys and occupancy;
- sleeping accommodation or vulnerable users;
- building height and complexity;
- current assessment date and significant changes;
- requested review, new assessment or remediation advice;
- timescale and responsible-person status.

This is also the evidence required to match a competent assessor to the property type.

### Drain unblocking

- exact postcode;
- internal fixture, private outside drain or suspected shared sewer;
- sewage backup, flooding or odour;
- affected fixtures and access;
- emergency timing;
- owner, tenant, landlord or business authority;
- ability to answer immediately.

Phone is primary. The form is overflow and triage.

### Boiler installation

- fuel and current boiler type;
- property type, bedrooms and bathrooms;
- boiler and flue position;
- hot-water requirements;
- like-for-like swap or system change;
- desired timing;
- postcode and survey availability.

Any finance question needs careful compliance review before publishing.

### House clearance

- postcode and property type;
- access, stairs, parking and keys;
- rooms or approximate volume;
- photographs;
- mattresses, refrigeration, electricals and hazardous items;
- items with reuse or resale potential;
- required date and authority to instruct.

The publisher can verify waste-carrier status before routing.

## Lead lifecycle

Use an append-only operational record:

```text
landing
→ call or form
→ contact attempt
→ connected
→ qualified
→ accepted or rejected with reason
→ appointment or quote
→ won or lost
→ revenue and contribution band
→ complaint credit or repeat
```

Never overwrite the raw event. Corrections should create a new state with actor, timestamp and reason. This makes billing, quality and privacy review possible.

## Routing

Start with capped exclusive routing. Each buyer has:

- accepted services and exclusions;
- postcodes or travel-time boundary;
- open hours and response SLA;
- weekly and daily cap;
- order of routing and overflow;
- credential status and renewal date;
- credit and suspension conditions.

Do not broadcast a consumer's details to several businesses unless the user was told clearly and the legal basis, experience and contract support it.

## Response time

For urgent calls, measure rings, connection, hold, abandonment and overflow. For forms, measure first attempt and actual contact. A site that ranks but hands a lead to voicemail is a failed acquisition product.

Commercial cleaning and loft conversions tolerate a longer response than drainage, but same-business-day discipline still affects close rate and trust.

## Search acquisition

Organic work begins with one transaction page, one evidence page and a small number of decision pages. Local links and citations should come from genuine relationships, suppliers, memberships, projects, local organisations and useful data—not bought ranking credit.

A bounded paid-search experiment can validate query quality and landing conversion before SEO matures. The media ceiling must be derived from accepted-lead or job contribution economics.

Affiliate or marketplace routing can validate demand where direct buyers are not ready. Bark's programme provides an observable path, but the payout and acceptance conditions must be tested rather than assumed.

## Tenant acquisition

Once real leads exist, sell the evidence:

- lead samples with personal data removed;
- geography and service mix;
- connected and accepted rates;
- response-time effect;
- appointment and quote outcomes;
- dispute and duplicate rate;
- weekly capacity fit;
- trial price and cap.

Do not sell traffic screenshots. Contractors buy profitable, serviceable work.

## Operational dashboard

The minimum weekly review includes:

| Layer     | Metrics                                                            |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Search    | impressions, clicks, landing page, query intent, AI visibility     |
| Contact   | calls, forms, connection, answer time, abandonment                 |
| Quality   | qualified, accepted, rejection reasons, duplicates, spam           |
| Sales     | appointment, quote, win, time to outcome                           |
| Economics | revenue per accepted lead, credit rate, contribution before labour |
| Supply    | buyer capacity, coverage, credential status, SLA failures          |
| Trust     | complaints, deletion requests, misleading-claim corrections        |

The lead-generation moat grows from this feedback loop. Search volume is public. A high-quality accepted-lead history is not.
