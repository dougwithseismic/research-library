# The UK Commercial-Premises Compliance Economy

Commercial-premises compliance is not one market. It is a set of duties, inspection regimes, evidence records, competent-person boundaries and remediation loops that happen to meet inside the same building. That distinction determines both the commercial opportunity and the safe use of AI.

## The decision

Test a **partner-led managed-compliance operation** for small and mid-sized property portfolios, beginning with fire-risk and asbestos workflows. The product should maintain a premises-and-obligation register, ingest original evidence, surface missing or conflicting records, coordinate verified providers, and keep actions open until an accountable person accepts valid completion evidence.

Do not begin as a generic compliance publisher, a certificate marketplace or a pure AI-reporting product. Generated information is abundant, search answers can remove informational visits, and established property platforms already generate forms, tickets and summaries. The scarce assets are premises history, field evidence, competent people, verified provider performance, controlled closure and the customer's trust.

AI belongs inside that operation, but its authority must stop before observation, professional judgement, certification or legal responsibility. It may extract, reconcile, draft, prioritise and communicate. It may not invent a site condition, approve a provider credential, declare a hazard resolved or sign an assessment.

## What the evidence says

- England and Wales had 2.14 million non-domestic rateable properties at 31 March 2026. These are rating units, not unique buildings, businesses or buyers, so they are a stock indicator rather than a serviceable-market estimate. [Official property-stock commentary](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/non-domestic-rating-stock-of-properties-march-2026/non-domestic-rating-stock-of-properties-statistical-commentary)
- England recorded 50,195 fire-safety audits in the year to March 2026; 20,286 required further action. The derived 40.4% unsatisfactory share is commercially relevant but cannot be applied to all premises because inspections are risk targeted. [Official fire-prevention statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/fire-prevention-and-protection-england-year-ending-march-2026/fire-prevention-and-protection-statistics-england-april-2025-to-march-2026)
- A required outcome does not always imply a required purchase. A simple, low-risk English business can sometimes complete its own fire-risk assessment, while more complex premises need competent help. Annual PAT testing of every appliance in a low-risk office is not a universal legal requirement. [Small-business fire guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/fire-safety-for-small-businesses-operating-from-commercial-premises) [HSE electrical-equipment guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/indg236.htm)
- Other lanes preserve much harder external boundaries. Covered gas work requires a suitably qualified Gas Safe registered engineer; qualifying lifting and pressure equipment requires competent examination; a business-premises EPC requires an accredited assessor. [HSE gas guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/gas/employers.htm) [HSE lifting-equipment guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/work-equipment-machinery/thorough-examinations-lifting-equipment.htm) [Business-premises EPC guidance](https://www.gov.uk/energy-performance-certificate-commercial-property)
- Historical keyword observations show explicit demand—8,100 average monthly UK searches for `fire risk assessment` and 6,600 for `asbestos survey`—but search volume is neither buyer count nor revenue. The retained demand rows and their limits are in the [evidence ledger](../evidence/evidence-ledger.csv).

## What AI changes

| Dimension                  |         Score | Commercial effect                                                                                    |
| -------------------------- | ------------: | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Generated-content supply   |  5/5 exposure | Advice, templates and location pages become cheap; content cannot be the core asset.                 |
| Discovery disruption       |  4/5 exposure | Answer layers compress informational visits; premises-specific transactions can persist.             |
| Agent bypass               |  3/5 exposure | Agents can shortlist providers and schedule work, bypassing thin lead brokers.                       |
| Operating leverage         | 4/5 advantage | Evidence extraction, report drafting, exception handling and coordination can reduce administration. |
| Proprietary-data potential | 4/5 advantage | A permissioned site–asset–obligation–finding–action–outcome graph can improve decisions over time.   |
| Physical-world moat        | 5/5 advantage | Hidden conditions, sampling, examination, maintenance and remediation remain physical.               |
| Trust and liability moat   | 5/5 advantage | Competence, independence, insurance, review and sign-off constrain unsafe automation.                |
| Defensibility in 2028      | 4/5 advantage | Managed operations can endure; standalone AI features are likely to be copied or bundled.            |

These scores are intentionally not combined. Some describe exposure and others describe advantage. The complete mechanism ledger is in [AI disruption](./06-ai-disruption-autonomy-and-the-2028-moat.md).

## The recommended entry sequence

1. Validate one portfolio buyer and one responsible-person workflow before building software.
2. Start with fire-risk administration and asbestos evidence because demand, urgency and external-provider routes are visible, while preserving each lane's different competence rules.
3. Run the first 50 sites through a manual evidence ledger and provider network.
4. Introduce AI in shadow mode for extraction and drafting only after an authorised, redacted evaluation corpus exists.
5. Release bounded reminders and evidence requests only when deterministic rules control dates and AI cannot close work.
6. Add legionella or lifting-equipment coordination only after the first lane retains customers and lane-specific expertise is available.
7. Consider acquisition only after job-level contribution, insurance, claims, credentials and owner dependence can be inspected confidentially.

The current weighted strategy score is 4.00/5 for partner-led managed compliance and 4.25/5 for acquiring an accredited operator. The latter remains deferred because a score cannot substitute for confidential diligence. Pure vertical SaaS scores 3.00/5; generic content and lead generation scores 2.25/5.
