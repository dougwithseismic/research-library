# The UK Commercial-Premises Compliance Economy

**Working subtitle:** What must be done, what must be bought, and what AI can actually change

**Status:** Internal working paper — framing

## Decision

Determine whether a defensible business should generate demand, manage compliance workflows, partner with accredited providers, acquire an operator, or remain out of the category.

## Autonomous research scope

- Map obligations by jurisdiction and distinguish required activity from required third-party spend
- Compare provider supply, credentials, public pricing, filed-company evidence, and software incumbents
- Model one-off, event-triggered, and recurring service economics
- Test AI-assisted administration, evidence review, triage, and reporting against liability and competence boundaries

## Human evidence seams

- Provider and buyer interviews
- Professional legal or regulatory interpretation
- Commercial terms with accredited providers
- Any live acquisition or paid-demand test

## Stop condition

Stop or narrow the thesis if external paid provision is not structurally necessary, credible providers cannot retain attractive contribution after acquisition cost, or liability cannot be bounded without owning regulated delivery.
