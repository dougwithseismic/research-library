# Which compliance lanes are commercially attractive?

## The answer

Use fire-risk administration as the opening workflow, pair it with asbestos evidence and survey coordination, and treat legionella and lifting equipment as the most plausible expansions. Defer pressure systems, gas and EPC work until the business has the corresponding technical authority and provider network. Exclude generic annual PAT sales from the thesis.

The ranking is about entry sequence, not the importance of each risk. It weights an externally valuable job, repeated evidence needs, physical or professional durability, data accumulation, reachable buyers, responsible AI scope and the cost of being wrong.

| Lane                | External spend                                       | Recurring operating work                      | AI leverage                             | Physical / trust moat | Entry decision                       |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Fire risk           | Conditional but common in complex premises           | High: reviews, change, actions, evidence      | High in capture, drafting and follow-up | High                  | Launch workflow                      |
| Asbestos            | Conditional survey; accredited analysis when sampled | High: register, plan, communication and works | High in indexing and conflict detection | Very high             | Launch companion                     |
| Legionella          | Conditional external competence                      | High where active controls exist              | High in records and anomaly triage      | High                  | Second wave                          |
| Lifting equipment   | Usually competent examination                        | High, with defined or scheme cadence          | High in asset and defect coordination   | Very high             | Second wave                          |
| Pressure systems    | Strong in qualifying systems                         | Scheme-defined                                | Moderate                                | Very high             | Specialist later lane                |
| Gas work            | Required qualified provider for covered work         | Asset and maintenance dependent               | Moderate                                | Very high             | Integrate later                      |
| EPC / MEES          | Accredited assessment when triggered                 | Event and policy dependent                    | Moderate                                | High                  | Portfolio module later               |
| Portable appliances | Risk-based maintenance                               | Variable                                      | Moderate                                | Low to moderate       | Do not sell a universal annual cycle |

## Why is fire risk the best opening workflow?

Fire risk combines five useful conditions.

First, the required outcome is clear enough to create urgency: the responsible person must assess and regularly review risk and record the assessment. Second, premises complexity produces a real route to external competence without supporting the false claim that every small business must buy the same assessment every year. Third, England's 2025–26 enforcement statistics show active audit and follow-up. Fourth, retained search data shows explicit public demand. Fifth, assessor work contains substantial document and action administration around an inescapable site visit.

Official England statistics report 50,195 audits, 20,286 unsatisfactory outcomes, 3,355 formal notifications, 1,896 enforcement notices and 1,224 prohibition notices. These are risk-targeted inspections, not a random estimate of national failure. [Official fire statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/fire-prevention-and-protection-england-year-ending-march-2026/fire-prevention-and-protection-statistics-england-april-2025-to-march-2026)

Retained read-only historical metrics show 8,100 average monthly UK searches for `fire risk assessment` and 1,300 for `fire risk assessment near me`. The rows may overlap through close variants and do not establish jobs or revenue. They do show that buyers use an explicit service phrase.

The opening product should solve four jobs:

1. identify the correct jurisdiction, premises context and responsible person;
2. collect existing reports, plans, material changes and unresolved findings before the visit;
3. give a competent assessor a structured brief and capture packet;
4. convert the accepted report into an accountable remediation queue.

AI should not produce a “free fire-risk assessment” from a chat. It can help a simple business navigate official guidance and decide whether to seek competent help, while making the boundary visible. Government is consulting on competence requirements and a central register for fire-risk assessors; that may improve provider verification but remains a proposal rather than current law. [Fire-assessor consultation](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/fire-risk-assessors-profession/fire-risk-assessors-profession)

### Fire economics to validate

Public comparison guidance indicates roughly £200–£700 for many small and medium commercial assessments, with complex properties higher. It is neither a tariff nor transaction microdata. Against the retained £9.10 Manchester local historical CPC, media-only cost per accepted lead is £303 at 3% accepted conversion, £130 at 7%, and £56.88 at 16%. [Indicative fire-risk pricing](https://www.fire-risk-assessment-quotes.co.uk/how-much-does-a-fire-risk-assessment-cost/)

The provider interviews must disclose actual site time, report time, review, travel, price, credit, acceptance and contribution. If a simple job cannot carry acquisition cost plus the managed layer, content-led demand should not be the entry model.

## Why add asbestos evidence early?

Asbestos is a better physical moat and a more complicated commercial product. The Great Britain duty to manage covers non-domestic premises, while the lease and maintenance responsibility affect who holds the duty. A dutyholder may already possess sufficient reliable information, so the system must not turn every address into a survey sale. [HSE duty guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/duty/check.htm)

Where surveying and sampling are necessary, site access, representative inspection and accredited analysis cannot be generated. HSE strongly recommends UKAS-accredited survey organisations and requires sample analysis by an accredited laboratory. [HSE survey guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/duty/arrange-asbestos-survey.htm)

The managed workflow has durable value before and after a survey:

- resolve ownership and maintenance responsibility;
- distinguish management from refurbishment or demolition contexts;
- find and index existing surveys and registers;
- identify inaccessible areas, assumptions and expired information;
- communicate the register to people planning intrusive work;
- route an appropriate survey where the evidence is insufficient;
- preserve samples, plans, material location and condition;
- track remedial or management actions without overstating certainty.

Retained search data shows 6,600 average monthly UK searches for `asbestos survey` and 1,000 for `asbestos survey near me`. A marketplace cost guide gives an indicative commercial average of £400, but the scope varies too widely to treat that as a unit price. [Indicative asbestos cost](https://www.checkatrade.com/blog/cost-guides/asbestos-survey-cost/)

The stop condition is conflict or poor economics: if survey partners will not expose scope, accreditation, response and completion evidence—or if the operator can earn only by encouraging unnecessary work—do not launch the lane.

## Where does legionella fit?

Legionella is attractive as a managed control loop rather than a certificate product. The person in control must assess risk, appoint someone with the necessary competence and authority, implement controls, monitor, retain records and review when circumstances change. A contractor can help but does not remove the dutyholder's responsibility. [HSE management guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/legionnaires/what-you-must-do/manage-the-risk.htm)

The workflow can include water-system identity, control scheme, temperature and flushing records, exceptions, access, responsible people, contractor evidence and change events. This supports anomaly detection only when the underlying system and sensor or check data are valid.

HSE warns against treating an ordinary landlord context as a universal annual test-and-certificate market. The public price source in this paper suggests £200–£500 for commercial assessments, with an average around £350, but that is a marketplace guide and does not resolve complex-system scope. [HSE landlord guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/legionnaires/legionella-landlords-responsibilities.htm) [Indicative pricing](https://www.checkatrade.com/blog/cost-guides/legionella-risk-assessment-cost/)

Launch only with an experienced water-safety authority who can define premises classes, control regimes, escalation and safe anomaly thresholds.

## Why is lifting equipment a strong second-wave lane?

Lifting equipment creates identifiable assets, periodic examinations, competent-person reports and defects that require follow-through. Normal intervals are six months for equipment that lifts people and lifting accessories and 12 months for other lifting equipment, unless a written examination scheme sets another interval. Exceptional events can create additional examinations. [HSE LOLER guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/work-equipment-machinery/thorough-examinations-lifting-equipment.htm)

The useful product joins asset identity to examination history, scheme basis, provider competence, defects, maintenance, exceptional events and closure evidence. AI can read reports, match serial numbers, detect missing intervals and draft action notices. It cannot classify every asset from a photograph, substitute maintenance for examination, perform the physical examination or decide independently that a serious defect is safe.

This lane can produce a better recurring model than generic fire leads, but only among portfolios that own enough in-scope assets. Interviews must measure whether existing maintenance or inspection platforms already own the workflow.

## Why defer pressure systems?

Pressure-systems work has excellent physical and professional durability. Qualifying systems require a suitable written scheme and examinations under that scheme by a competent person. Applicability depends on equipment and relevant fluid, while the scheme sets the parts, nature and frequency of examination. [HSE PSSR guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pressure-systems/pssr.htm)

That specificity makes it a poor generic launch lane. A wrong classification or omitted component has high consequence. The managed operator should add it only with a named technical authority, provider redundancy and an evaluation set covering real schemes and reports.

## Why integrate gas later?

Gas work preserves external provision because covered work must be completed by a suitably qualified Gas Safe registered engineer. The software opportunity is asset data, evidence, scope and scheduling, not the work itself. [HSE gas guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/gas/employers.htm)

It is not the opening lane because domestic annual-landlord language can be misapplied to commercial premises, plant and contract contexts. A safe product needs the exact equipment, work category and registered scope. It can become valuable when a portfolio already trusts the service across assets.

## Why is EPC a later portfolio module?

Non-domestic EPCs provide structured, accredited outputs and public policy attention. Covered private rented premises in England and Wales currently generally need EPC E or an exemption. The announced 2031 EPC B intention applies to rented buildings over 1,000 square metres and needs legislation. [Current MEES guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/non-domestic-private-rented-property-minimum-energy-efficiency-standard-landlord-guidance) [Interim response](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/non-domestic-private-rented-sector-minimum-energy-efficiency-standards-epc-b-implementation/outcome/minimum-energy-efficiency-standards-mees-in-the-non-domestic-private-rented-sector-interim-response)

The lane is less naturally recurring because EPC production is tied to validity, transaction and building events. Its value is higher in portfolio planning: find certificates, validate identity, model likely upgrade paths, coordinate assessors and preserve improvement evidence. The accredited assessor must direct and verify data collection; remote generation from unverified customer data is unacceptable. [Non-dwelling EPC guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/energy-performance-certificates-for-the-construction-sale-and-let-of-non-dwellings--2/a-guide-to-energy-performance-certificates-for-the-construction-sale-and-let-of-non-dwellings)

## What should be excluded?

Exclude three propositions:

- **Universal annual PAT:** the official guidance rejects the premise in low-risk environments.
- **AI-generated certification:** language quality cannot create observation, competence or accreditation.
- **One national obligation template:** fire jurisdictions differ and other regimes have their own geographical boundaries.

Also exclude any remediation recommendation optimised for referral revenue. The system must separate a statutory requirement, assessor recommendation, company policy and commercial suggestion.

## The lane decision

Start with fire-risk evidence and action coordination because it is legible to buyers and providers. Add asbestos only with correctly scoped accredited partners and strict conflicts control. Use what is learned about registers, assets, findings and closure to evaluate legionella and lifting equipment. Pressure, gas and energy remain integrations until specialist competence and real portfolio demand exist.
