# How I would enter commercial-premises compliance

I would build a **managed evidence and coordination service for commercial-property portfolios**, not an AI compliance oracle.

The first product would give a property manager one reliable view of premises, dutyholder context, assets, assessments, findings, actions, provider credentials and completion evidence. It would begin with fire-risk and asbestos workflows, fulfilled through competent external partners. A human operator would resolve exceptions, approve routing and protect the distinction between an uploaded document, an accepted finding and valid closure.

AI would reduce the cost of turning messy records into an operating queue. It would extract fields with source links, reconcile dates and addresses, draft reports from accepted observations, identify missing evidence, propose follow-up questions and prepare communications. It would not conduct an inspection, infer a hidden material from a photograph, certify compliance or inherit the responsible person's duty.

That is a less theatrical AI proposition than “automated compliance,” but it is more commercially credible. The work combines abundant administration with irreducibly physical and professional steps. AI compresses the first part; it does not abolish the second.

## Why enter through operations rather than content?

Generic compliance information is structurally weak as a core business. Guidance, checklists, template assessments and location pages are cheap to generate. Google says generative AI may support research and structure but warns against scaled pages that add little value. Answer layers can also resolve a basic question without sending the reader to a publisher. A March 2025 US browsing panel found traditional-result clicks on 8% of visits with an AI summary versus 15% without one; that is not a UK compliance conversion study, but it establishes a plausible discovery mechanism. [Google guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content?hl=en) [Pew click study](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)

The transactional question is harder: which duty applies to this premises, who owns it, what evidence already exists, what changed, which competent provider can act, and what proves the work is complete? Those answers require local records, current law, physical observations, provider availability and accountable decisions. A managed operator can own that loop even when a search or workplace agent becomes the interface.

Content still has a role. It can acquire customers, explain evidence requirements and prepare a buyer to provide a useful brief. It becomes an acquisition surface for the operating system, not the asset on which the company depends.

## Why begin with fire risk and asbestos?

Fire risk has visible obligation, search demand, regulatory attention and a fragmented provider base. England's official year-to-March-2026 data records 50,195 fire-safety audits, 20,286 of which were unsatisfactory and required further action. The audits were risk targeted, so 40.4% is not an estimate for all premises. It still shows that assessment, evidence and remediation are live operating work rather than a theoretical rule. [Fire prevention and protection statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/fire-prevention-and-protection-england-year-ending-march-2026/fire-prevention-and-protection-statistics-england-april-2025-to-march-2026)

Asbestos creates a complementary evidence problem. The duty to manage can sit with an owner, landlord, tenant or managing agent according to maintenance and repair responsibility. Some dutyholders can rely on existing reliable information, while sampling and intrusive knowledge move into competent, often accredited provision. Registers and management plans then need to remain accessible to anyone who may disturb the material. [HSE dutyholder guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/duty/check.htm) [HSE survey guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/duty/arrange-asbestos-survey.htm)

The two lanes should not be blended into one “compliance certificate.” Fire law differs across England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Asbestos guidance in this paper is principally Great Britain guidance. The system must preserve jurisdiction, premises class, source version, provider competence and responsibility per lane.

The detailed entry sequence for each obligation is in the [compliance lane dossiers](./04-compliance-lane-dossiers.md).

## What exactly would the customer buy?

The launch service would have five components.

### 1. Premises and responsibility register

Each site receives a controlled profile:

- address and building identity;
- jurisdiction and premises use;
- occupancy and vulnerable-person context;
- owner, occupier, employer and managing-agent roles;
- lease or contract evidence that affects maintenance responsibility;
- in-scope assets and known exclusions;
- material changes, planned works and transaction triggers.

The product must allow “unknown.” A confident but incorrect dutyholder allocation is worse than an explicit gap routed for professional review.

### 2. Evidence ingestion

Original PDFs, photographs, plans, certificates, registers and provider reports remain immutable source records. Extracted fields point to the exact document and page or span. Human-approved observations become separate records rather than overwriting the source.

The important distinction is:

```text
uploaded document ≠ verified evidence ≠ accepted finding ≠ completed action
```

That prevents an expired, mismatched or draft document from becoming “green” merely because it exists in the folder.

### 3. Obligation and exception queue

A deterministic rules layer proposes which records, reviews or examinations may apply and exposes the inputs behind the result. AI can translate an operator's free text into structured facts and retrieve source passages; it cannot silently select the jurisdiction or turn guidance into a universal deadline.

Exceptions receive more attention than routine dates:

- no responsible person identified;
- address or asset mismatch;
- missing original report;
- provider credential outside its relevant scope;
- overdue high-consequence action;
- evidence conflicts across two documents;
- material change since the last assessment;
- proposed closure without completion evidence.

### 4. Provider coordination

The service obtains a premises-specific brief, shortlists providers by competence, geography, insurance, capacity and conflict, and records why a provider was selected. A human approves the appointment. At least two qualified options should exist per active lane and region before the operation promises coverage.

Companies House can help resolve the legal entity and inspect filed accounts, but it does not prove current trading quality, competence, capacity, margin or willingness to partner. The retained Manchester fire-provider sample contains businesses with one to 76 filed employees, with turnover mostly undisclosed. It is supply evidence, not a verified network.

### 5. Action and closure loop

Every confirmed finding becomes an action with source, severity, owner, due basis, uncertainty and expected evidence. AI may draft the task and chase the owner. It may assemble a completion packet. Only a named accountable person can accept closure, and the record must retain who accepted what, when, against which evidence.

## Where does AI create economic value?

The first economic effect is administrative leverage for providers. Consider an illustrative simple assessment with four hours on site and four hours writing. If controlled drafting reduces only report time by 20%, total time falls from eight to 7.2 hours, an 11.1% theoretical capacity increase. At 40% report-time reduction, total time is 6.4 hours and theoretical capacity rises 25%.

A fire-software vendor claims 62% less report-writing time. Applying that claim to the same illustration produces 5.52 total hours and 44.9% theoretical capacity uplift. That is an unverified ceiling, not a forecast: travel, review, sales, demand, site complexity and scheduling remain. [FireCheckr](https://firecheckr.co.uk/)

The second effect is portfolio coordination. AI can process documents and communications across many sites without one operator reading each file from scratch. Yet the savings belong to whoever controls price and workflow. A provider may turn saved time into more jobs. A managed operator may reduce service cost. A customer may demand a lower price. An incumbent platform may include the feature in its existing subscription. The business case must measure contribution after that distribution, not advertise “hours saved” as if all saved hours became profit.

The third effect is better follow-through. Faster drafting matters less than preventing an unresolved high-consequence action from disappearing between a report, an inbox and a contractor. The product should measure overdue critical actions and false closures before pages generated or tokens consumed.

## Why is an AI report writer not enough?

Vertical report drafting is already visible in the market. FireCheckr markets voice and photo capture into draft fire-risk reports. Assura Safety markets AI-drafted, cited action plans from £125 plus VAT per user per month and explicitly leaves responsibility with the assessor. Horizontal products are broader: PlanRadar offers form generation, voice-to-ticket, document search, summaries and agents; SafetyCulture includes AI template generation; MaintainX applies AI to procedures, document retrieval and maintenance anomalies. [Assura Safety](https://assurasafety.com/) [PlanRadar AI](https://www.planradar.ai/) [SafetyCulture pricing](https://safetyculture.com/pricing/) [MaintainX AI](https://help.getmaintainx.com/ai-basics)

The implication is not that these products solve every UK compliance problem. It is that extraction, template creation and fluent report prose will be features, not a defensible company by themselves.

The managed operator must earn assets the model vendor and generic workflow platform do not automatically have:

- permissioned multi-site records with durable provenance;
- a resolved provider graph covering person, entity, scope, geography and credentials;
- evidence about response, acceptance, defect, remediation and recurrence;
- direct buyer relationships and a trusted exception service;
- lane-specific rules and evaluation sets approved by competent reviewers;
- contracts that allocate responsibility, data rights and escalation;
- operational ability to get physical work completed.

## What is the safe autonomy ladder?

The product should make autonomy explicit.

| Level           | AI authority                                             | Launch position                                       |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 0 — retrieve    | Find source records and applicable guidance              | Allowed with citations and versioning                 |
| 1 — extract     | Propose fields from documents, images and messages       | Allowed with field provenance and review              |
| 2 — draft       | Prepare templates, narrative, actions and communications | Shadow first; competent review required               |
| 3 — recommend   | Rank exceptions, providers or actions                    | Advisory; expose reasons and uncertainty              |
| 4 — bounded act | Create reminders, request evidence and open tasks        | Allowed only under deterministic rules and audit logs |
| 5 — attest      | Declare compliance, close risk or sign professionally    | Prohibited                                            |

The phrase “human in the loop” is too vague. The assessor must accept site observations and final report content. The provider-operations lead must approve credentials and routing. The responsible person must accept closure. The technical authority must approve obligation rules and templates. Each release threshold names the human and the event they control.

The complete implementation boundary and test suite are specified in [AI control architecture and evaluation](./07-ai-control-architecture-and-evaluation.md).

## What would the launch economics need to prove?

Historical Google Ads data for a Manchester fire-risk cluster produced a £9.10 volume-weighted CPC. If 3% of clicks become accepted leads, media alone costs about £303 per accepted lead. At 7%, it is £130; at 16%, £56.88. Public comparison-site guidance places many small and medium commercial fire-risk assessments around £200–£700, but this is indicative price content, not transaction data. [Commercial fire-risk price guide](https://www.fire-risk-assessment-quotes.co.uk/how-much-does-a-fire-risk-assessment-cost/)

That arithmetic makes paid lead generation fragile. At the conservative conversion case, media could consume most of a simple job's headline price before delivery. Even the base case requires provider contribution, credit policy, call handling and acceptance to be measured. The launch should therefore come through design partners, direct portfolio sales and provider relationships before paid search.

The managed-service scenarios are hypotheses rather than forecasts:

| Case         | Sites | Monthly fee per site | Annual platform revenue |
| ------------ | ----: | -------------------: | ----------------------: |
| Conservative |    50 |                  £49 |                 £29,400 |
| Base         |   150 |                  £79 |                £142,200 |
| Upside       |   500 |                  £99 |                £594,000 |

Third-party assessments and remediation are excluded. So are human exception cost, sales cycle, support, integrations and churn. The first pricing test asks whether customers will pay for the evidence-and-coordination layer separately from provider fees.

[Business models and unit economics](./08-business-models-and-unit-economics.md) shows the full scenario set and the acquisition diligence boundary.

## Why not buy an operator immediately?

Owning delivery has the highest strategic score because it combines demand, practitioner trust, field evidence, provider economics and AI leverage. It also creates the greatest hidden-risk surface.

An acquisition case requires at least three years of accounts, job-level contribution, customer concentration, insurance and claims history, professional credentials, software contracts, report quality, outstanding actions, owner dependence, staff retention and the transferability of referrals. Public accounts cannot resolve those questions. AI savings should not be valued until a controlled time-and-quality study proves them.

Partnership is the reversible route. It exposes real records, responsibility seams, margins and buyer behaviour without paying for earnings whose quality is unknown.

## What would make me stop?

I would stop or narrow the company if any of these holds after the bounded validation programme:

- property teams say their problem is fully solved by ordinary calendars and document storage;
- no named budget owner will delegate coordination or share original evidence;
- fewer than two credible providers per lane and region will agree usable service, data and responsibility terms;
- accepted-lead cost consumes provider contribution under conservative assumptions;
- AI fails any critical rule, credential, observation, action or closure threshold;
- competent review removes the supposed time saving;
- customers, rather than the operator, retain all savings without paying for better follow-through;
- existing property or maintenance platforms can satisfy the exact workflow with no switching reason;
- liability cannot be bounded without employing the regulated or competent delivery role;
- the site data cannot be retained and reused lawfully enough to improve the service.

## The recommendation

Proceed to a 50-site, partner-led design programme. Sell evidence control and accountable coordination, not automated compliance. Keep the first AI releases in retrieval, extraction and drafting; release bounded agents only after deterministic rules and zero-tolerance safety tests pass. Defer pure SaaS and acquisition until real customer, provider, quality and contribution evidence changes the decision.
