# Market, duties and the procurement boundary

## What is the market?

The commercial-premises compliance economy is the flow of information, inspection, professional judgement, remediation and assurance created by buildings, assets and legal responsibilities. It is not the number of businesses that can be found in a directory and it is not a catalogue of certificates.

The practical value chain is:

```text
law, guidance and standards
  → dutyholder and premises interpretation
    → inventory and evidence
      → assessment or examination
        → finding and action
          → remediation
            → completion evidence
              → review, change trigger or recurrence
```

Several businesses can participate: property manager, facilities team, assessor, surveyor, engineer, software vendor, contractor, insurer, training provider and lead generator. The duty usually remains with a named person or organisation even when a contractor performs work.

## How large is the installed environment?

England and Wales contained 2.14 million non-domestic rateable properties at 31 March 2026. The distribution was:

| VOA sector | Rateable properties | Share of stated stock |
| ---------- | ------------------: | --------------------: |
| Retail     |             512,500 |                 23.9% |
| Office     |             428,270 |                 20.0% |
| Industrial |             566,240 |                 26.5% |
| Other      |             631,250 |                 29.5% |

The percentages are derived from the official counts and may not sum perfectly because of rounding. The `other` category includes diverse property types with very different duties and buying behaviour. Sixty-three per cent of the total had rateable value at or below £12,000, which warns against assuming enterprise budgets across the stock. [Official property-stock commentary](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/non-domestic-rating-stock-of-properties-march-2026/non-domestic-rating-stock-of-properties-statistical-commentary)

No serviceable-market total is stated. To reach one, the operator would need to resolve unique sites, occupation, relevant assets, dutyholder, current evidence, trigger, purchase necessity, existing contract and reachable budget.

## Which duties create an external purchase?

### Fire risk: mandatory outcome, conditional external provider

In England, the responsible person must carry out and regularly review a fire-risk assessment and record it. Government guidance for small, simple, low-risk premises supports competent self-assessment; complex premises or insufficient expertise create the need for external help. When appointing someone, government guidance recommends checking competence, registration or third-party certification and insurance. [Workplace fire-risk guidance](https://www.gov.uk/workplace-fire-safety-your-responsibilities/fire-risk-assessments) [Choosing help](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/people-with-duties-under-fire-safety-laws/a-guide-for-persons-with-duties-under-fire-safety-legislation-accessible)

Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland need their own rules and wording. A UK product cannot take an England template, switch the address and assume it remains correct. [Welsh guidance](https://www.gov.wales/fire-safety-guidance-businesses-and-workplaces) [Scottish guidance](https://www.gov.scot/policies/fire-and-rescue/non-domestic-fire-safety/) [Northern Ireland guidance](https://www.nifrs.org/home/staying-safe/business-fire-safety/fire-risk-assessments/)

Commercially, fire risk supports both self-service and external provision. The stronger offer is not “everyone must buy an annual assessment.” It is correct scoping, evidence control, competent escalation and action follow-through.

### Asbestos: duty to manage, conditional survey, accredited analysis

The Great Britain duty to manage applies to non-domestic premises and common parts of multi-occupied domestic premises. Responsibility can follow control of maintenance and repair under the lease or contract. The dutyholder must determine whether asbestos-containing material is present or likely, assess condition and exposure risk, and manage it. [HSE dutyholder check](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/duty/check.htm)

An external survey is not automatically the first step in every case: reliable existing information may be enough for some decisions. Where a survey or sampling is needed, competence matters. HSE strongly recommends UKAS-accredited surveying organisations, and sample analysis must use an accredited laboratory. [HSE survey guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/duty/arrange-asbestos-survey.htm)

This lane creates durable physical work—inspection, sampling and analysis—but it also creates a conflict risk. A commercial system must not recommend unnecessary surveys or remediation simply because it earns from them.

### Electrical equipment: maintenance duty, no universal annual PAT sale

HSE says electrical equipment must be maintained to prevent danger but that the law does not require every portable appliance in a low-risk environment to be tested annually. User checks, visual inspections and testing depend on equipment and risk. [HSE guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/indg236.htm)

The commercial conclusion is negative: an “annual PAT certificate for every office” factory would convert a myth into recurring revenue. A defensible workflow schedules proportionate maintenance and records why a control applies.

### Gas: qualified physical work

For covered gas work in shops, restaurants, schools and hospitals, HSE states that a suitably qualified Gas Safe registered engineer must do the work. The exact qualification must fit the equipment and activity. [HSE gas guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/gas/employers.htm)

This preserves provider demand but narrows the software role. AI can identify assets, retrieve records, prepare a job and reconcile the engineer's evidence. It cannot perform registered work or turn a generic contractor record into verified scope.

### Legionella: managed control, not a universal certificate

An employer or person in control must assess and manage legionella risk and appoint a competent responsible person. Using a contractor does not transfer the underlying responsibility. The appropriate control depends on the water system. [HSE risk-management guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/legionnaires/what-you-must-do/manage-the-risk.htm)

HSE's landlord guidance says there is no statutory legionella test certificate and no fixed annual or biennial assessment interval in the ordinary context it describes. Complex systems such as cooling towers, healthcare environments and spas demand different treatment. [Landlord guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/legionnaires/legionella-landlords-responsibilities.htm) [HSE management bulletin](https://www.hse.gov.uk/safetybulletins/legionella2.htm)

This is a good coordination lane but a dangerous marketing lane. The service should sell ongoing control evidence and competent escalation, not an invented universal certificate.

### Lifting equipment: repeated competent examination

LOLER thorough examinations are normally required every six months for equipment lifting people and lifting accessories, every 12 months for other lifting equipment, or under another interval set by a written examination scheme. A competent person needs practical and theoretical knowledge and sufficient independence. [HSE lifting-equipment guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/work-equipment-machinery/thorough-examinations-lifting-equipment.htm)

This is one of the strongest repeated external-work lanes, but equipment classification, exceptional events, maintenance and examination must remain distinct. The buyer is more likely to value asset history and provider coordination than generic advice.

### Pressure systems: scheme-defined competent examination

Qualifying pressure equipment requires a suitable written scheme before use and examination under that scheme by a competent person. Not every vessel or fluid falls within scope. [HSE pressure-systems guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pressure-systems/pssr.htm)

The physical and professional moat is high. So is the cost of getting the rule wrong. This should be a later lane with qualified domain ownership rather than a launch template generated from public guidance.

### EPC and MEES: accredited output, event and policy triggers

An EPC for business premises in England and Wales must be produced by an accredited non-domestic energy assessor. Remote lodgement using information not collected or verified under the assessor's direction is not acceptable. [Business-premises EPC guidance](https://www.gov.uk/energy-performance-certificate-commercial-property) [Non-dwelling EPC guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/energy-performance-certificates-for-the-construction-sale-and-let-of-non-dwellings--2/a-guide-to-energy-performance-certificates-for-the-construction-sale-and-let-of-non-dwellings)

Covered privately rented non-domestic property generally needs an EPC rating of E or a valid exemption under current minimum-energy-efficiency rules. In June 2026 the government stated an intention to move rented buildings over 1,000 square metres to EPC B from 2031 and not proceed with a 2027 EPC C milestone. That depends on secondary legislation and is not current law. [Current MEES guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/non-domestic-private-rented-property-minimum-energy-efficiency-standard-landlord-guidance) [2026 interim response](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/non-domestic-private-rented-sector-minimum-energy-efficiency-standards-epc-b-implementation/outcome/minimum-energy-efficiency-standards-mees-in-the-non-domestic-private-rented-sector-interim-response)

EPC work is event driven and policy sensitive. The opportunity is portfolio evidence, upgrade planning and assessor coordination, not autonomous lodgement.

## What repeats and what does not?

The useful recurring object is not always the assessment. It can be:

- a scheduled examination under a defined cadence;
- a review triggered by change, deterioration or new information;
- a persistent register that must be communicated;
- monitoring readings or routine checks;
- unresolved remediation actions;
- expiring provider or insurance evidence;
- transaction or tenancy evidence;
- a new asset, tenant, layout, occupancy or building use.

This supports recurring managed revenue without fabricating recurring statutory purchases. The software calendar should store the basis for every due event: statute, approved scheme, assessor recommendation, manufacturer instruction, company policy, contract or inferred reminder. Those bases must never be presented as interchangeable.

## Who is the buyer?

The dutyholder and budget owner may differ. Likely buyers include:

- commercial property managers with multi-site evidence fragmentation;
- facilities leads responsible for assets and contractors;
- owners or occupiers without an internal safety team;
- managing agents coordinating landlord and tenant responsibilities;
- specialist providers seeking cleaner briefs and less report administration;
- insurers or advisers that require evidence but do not perform the duty.

The initial customer should manage enough sites for coordination to hurt but not already have a deeply integrated enterprise platform and national facilities contract. That segment is an inference until interviews and paid design partnerships validate it.

## Where does value accumulate?

The most durable value pools are:

1. interpreting premises and responsibility correctly;
2. capturing physical observations and samples;
3. providing competent, independent professional work;
4. resolving a provider's legal entity, person, scope and current credentials;
5. coordinating remediation and preserving valid evidence;
6. maintaining a longitudinal property and asset history;
7. detecting exceptions before an audit, transaction or incident.

Generic advice and document prose sit upstream of those pools and face the greatest AI compression. Certification without competent delivery is not a value pool; it is a liability.

## The market conclusion

There is a credible economy, but not a single annual-certificate market. The entry opportunity sits between fragmented customer records and lane-specific competent delivery. Build the obligation map to route work safely, then earn recurring revenue from evidence, exceptions and follow-through. Do not multiply 2.14 million rating units by a list price and call the result a market.
