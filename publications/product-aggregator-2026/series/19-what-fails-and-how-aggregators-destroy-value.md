# What fails and how aggregators destroy value

## What is the direct answer?

Product aggregators most often destroy value in five moves:

1. they scale imported pages before proving a decision advantage;
2. they mistake distribution for an owned customer relationship;
3. they enter fulfilment, inventory or payments because the adjacent revenue pool looks larger;
4. they let evidence scope grow faster than the funding and update system;
5. they quote gross price, traffic or transaction value while ignoring the path that determines payable net revenue.

The [failure-case ledger](../evidence/failure-case-ledger.csv) contains 12 observed patterns and explicit interpretation boundaries. Not every case is a failed company. A live and valuable operator can still expose a failure mechanism in one expansion, channel or commercial path.

## 1. Feed scale creates maintenance before it creates a moat

A product feed makes publication look nearly free. It contains names, descriptions, identifiers, prices, stock, images and deep links. The apparent opportunity is to render every category and combination.

Google's spam policies explicitly classify copied descriptions and cookie-cutter affiliate pages without added value as thin affiliation. It points instead to original prices, tests, ratings, navigation and comparison. [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)

The deeper failure is economic, not only algorithmic. Every imported row creates:

- a product-identity problem;
- a variant and bundle problem;
- a freshness promise;
- a specification-conflict problem;
- an indexable page that can become stale;
- a customer-support risk if the site implies more certainty than it has.

If the page does not help a buyer exclude an unsuitable product, complete a system, understand price history or select a trustworthy seller, its maintenance liability may exceed its decision value.

**Stop rule:** do not index a product or comparison page until it passes a value test: unique evidence, unique decision rule, useful cross-offer history, or a required node in a saved project.

## 2. Search distribution is neither neutral nor permanent

The PriceRunner case is unusually direct evidence. In July 2026, Sweden's Patent and Market Court announced a first-instance decision finding that Google unlawfully favoured its own comparison-shopping service and awarded damages to PriceRunner. Klarna described the award as $1.97 billion including accrued interest. The judgment may be appealed; it is not appropriate to treat the amount as final collected value. [Court announcement](https://www.domstol.se/nyheter/2026/07/google-is-to-pay-damages-to-pricerunner/), [Klarna announcement](https://investors.klarna.com/News--Events/news/news-details/2026/Klarna-has-received-a-favorable-ruling-in-PriceRunner-litigation-awarding-damages-of-1-97-billion-2026-e4mzixyA9j/default.aspx)

The strategic conclusion does not depend on the final award. An independent comparison service can be exposed to the ranking and product design of the platform that supplies its demand while also competing with it.

Affiliate publishers face a related but broader problem. Search engines can answer the query, shopping surfaces can render merchant offers and agents can synthesize feeds without recreating the publisher's journey. The publisher may still influence the sale but lose the click that pays for it.

**Stop rule:** no channel should account for the thesis. Track the percentage of useful sessions, saved projects and revenue attributable to each search engine, email, direct visit, referral, partner and agent. If the company cannot reach a saved user without reacquiring them, it does not own the relationship.

## 3. Owning the transaction can be worse than informing it

CarGurus remains a major vehicle marketplace and dealer product. Its 2025 Form 10-K also records a sharp boundary. The board decided in August 2025 to wind down the CarOffer transaction business, and the wind-down was completed or the relevant operation abandoned by the end of the year. The filing says the transaction model was less effective amid volatile pricing and dealer demand for flexibility and automation. [CarGurus 2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1494259/000119312526059435/carg-20251231.htm)

The filing records material charges across the period, including $115.2 million of CarOffer goodwill impairment in 2024, $19.6 million of goodwill impairment in 2025 and $13.3 million of 2025 wind-down expenditure, alongside intangible and software impairments. Accounting charges are not the same as total cash loss, but they establish that the adjacency consumed real capital.

The working marketplace assets—consumer traffic, deal ratings, dealer subscriptions, lead products and market data—did not make inventory transaction execution automatically attractive.

**Stop rule:** the proposed aggregator should not buy stock, guarantee resale value, become merchant of record or finance an installed project merely because the gross revenue is larger. It should first prove that control of the transaction improves conversion or margin enough to fund working capital, fraud, logistics, returns and support.

## 4. Commerce can be operationally separable from the decision platform

Houzz began as home inspiration and professional discovery, added a product marketplace and later built Houzz Pro. Its current terms state that Shop Houzz is operated by a Cart.com entity under licence; that entity acts as merchant of record and handles customer service and returns. [Houzz terms](https://www.houzz.com/termsOfUse)

The terms do not say why the structure exists, whether an earlier model failed or what the licensed economics are. They do establish that the product-commerce operation is separable from Houzz's project, professional and software layers. Houzz Pro is currently sold as an all-in-one professional product with paid monthly pricing. [Houzz Pro pricing](https://www.houzz.com/houzz-pro/pricing?proType=core)

The founder lesson is not “commerce failed”. It is that inspiration, professional workflow and merchant-of-record retail require different operations. Combining them in a pitch deck does not create operational synergy.

**Stop rule:** keep checkout with retailers and installed fulfilment with suppliers until the retained project and supplier data prove a specific reason to internalise another layer.

## 5. A strategic acquisition does not validate the standalone model forever

Etsy announced an agreement to acquire Reverb for $275 million cash in 2019, arguing that its marketplace expertise could extend into musical instruments. Etsy sold Reverb in June 2025 for $105 million cash; its filing recorded fair-value consideration of $108.2 million, a carrying amount of $113.3 million and a $5.1 million accounting loss on sale. Reverb continues as a standalone specialist marketplace. [2019 acquisition announcement](https://investors.etsy.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/115/etsy-to-acquire-reverb-a-leading-online-marketplace-for-new-used-and-vintage-musical-instruments), [Etsy 2025 Form 10-K](https://investors.etsy.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001370637-26-000019/etsy-20251231.htm), [Reverb ownership announcement](https://reverb.com/de/news/reverb-servco-creator-partners)

It would be wrong to call the $170 million difference a loss. The public figures do not capture Reverb's interim cash flow, investment, financing, tax, integration costs or strategic benefit. The valid conclusion is narrower: a credible specialist marketplace can cease to fit a parent's portfolio, and acquisition price is not permanent proof of strategic value.

**Stop rule:** test ownership fit separately from product fit. A garden decision company should have one coherent buyer, evidence system and supplier network before it acquires unrelated “high-ticket” categories for page or revenue growth.

## 6. Evidence breadth can weaken the evidence company

Which's current method page says it tests almost 4,000 products a year across more than 100 categories, but also explains when it stops testing: low member interest, obsolescence or reduced usefulness. Its annual report recorded membership down 4.1% at June 2025. [Which test-selection policy](https://www.which.co.uk/about-which/why-we-test-what-we-test-amoEV2R0joh0), [Which 2025 annual report](https://media.product.which.co.uk/prod/files/file/gm-1ece30e6-0c1d-4839-85ff-dc37544931ae-ar-2025-1.pdf)

One year's membership change is not proof of structural decline, and no category-level economics are public. The case still reveals the portfolio problem. Tests have different acquisition cost, update cadence, storage burden, expert requirement and member value. Coverage can become an obligation long after it ceases to change a decision.

**Stop rule:** each category has a documented `continue`, `pause` or `retire` decision based on user demand, decision value, usable life, update burden and revenue contribution. Retired results remain dated records, not silently current verdicts.

## 7. A scaled publisher still has to escape anonymous sessions

Future's HY2026 results reported group revenue down 8% and organic revenue down 6%. The company simultaneously described growth in direct advertising and said 16% of total revenue was directly correlated with website sessions. Its preceding full-year results highlighted Future+, Signal and Collab as routes into direct members, email, social, creator content and new commerce surfaces. [Future results hub](https://futureplc.com/investor-results-events/), [Future FY2025 results](https://futureplc.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Future-plc-2025-FULL-YEAR-RESULTS.pdf)

Group results cannot be assigned to one product-review brand or to search traffic alone. The evidence does show management allocating effort away from a model dominated by anonymous page sessions.

The failure pattern is gradual: search traffic looks like an asset because it can be monetised for years, while the user record, distribution permission and transaction evidence remain with other platforms.

**Stop rule:** measure known-user usefulness, not newsletter-box conversion. A saved garden, price target, product shortlist, service record or maintenance reminder should justify the relationship.

## 8. Platform-specific utilities can be durable and fragile at the same time

camelcamelcamel has a crisp product: Amazon price history and price-drop alerts. Its About page also states that it participates in Amazon Associates and that Amazon-supplied content is provided as-is and can change or be removed. [camelcamelcamel About](https://camelcamelcamel.com/about)

Keepa has built a deeper paid data layer around Amazon product history, offers, deals, sellers, alerts and API access. That depth can be commercially valuable while leaving the underlying product universe concentrated in one platform. [Keepa API](https://keepa.com/api-docs/)

The lesson is not to avoid platform-specific products. It is to price dependency honestly and preserve whatever may lawfully and contractually remain portable: user relationship, independently observed history, methods, derived statistics and workflow.

**Stop rule:** every critical upstream dependency has an owner, contract or terms reference, failure mode, replacement route and minimum viable degraded product.

## 9. Trust promises without trust operations create hidden liability

Discogs has an unusually valuable contributor-built identity graph. Its Buyer Policy also says third-party sellers are not pre-screened and that Discogs is not the seller or guarantor. [Discogs database architecture](https://support.discogs.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008545114-Overview-Of-How-Discogs-Is-Built), [buyer policy](https://support.discogs.com/hc/en-us/articles/14587773391501-Buyer-Policy)

This illustrates three layers that are often collapsed:

```text
catalogue truth ≠ seller trust ≠ transaction protection
```

A platform can be exceptional at the first and deliberately limited at the other two. Problems begin when interface language causes the buyer to assume all three.

**Stop rule:** use precise labels. `Identity checked`, `insurance document current`, `manufacturer-accredited`, `project reviewed`, `payment protected` and `outcome observed` are different claims with different evidence and expiry.

## 10. High prices can hide weak payable economics

Waltons' observed affiliate terms show 1.5% commission and exclude orders placed through finance. Machine Mart reports more than 21,000 products but a whole-site average order above only £100. One Garden reports a £314 AOV for a broad garden assortment. [Waltons terms](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/3314), [Machine Mart](https://www.machinemart.co.uk/affiliates/), [One Garden programme](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/109432)

These are useful negative observations. A visible £5,000 cabin, £3,000 tool or £2,000 spa does not establish the average payable publisher transaction.

The calculation has to follow the path:

```text
eligible click
× merchant conversion
× eligible transaction share
× commissionable order value
× headline rate
× validation rate
× attribution retention
= expected publisher revenue
```

Every unknown should remain a variable rather than being replaced by the most flattering visible number.

**Stop rule:** no category receives scale investment until at least 30 attributable transactions or accepted leads expose the actual distribution, reversals, delay and route exclusions. Thirty is a learning threshold, not statistical proof.

## What failure pattern matters most for this proposal?

The greatest risk is building the broad premium-garden publication before the decision engine.

The vertical has enough topics and merchant products to generate hundreds of plausible pages. That makes premature scale easy. It could produce traffic and even affiliate sales while leaving no owned compatibility, price history, test data, project state or installer outcome. The business would look active but remain substitutable.

The correct early constraint is severe:

- one property schema;
- one robotic-mower test bench;
- one product identity graph;
- one price-history record;
- one structured retailer or installer brief;
- one saved-project loop.

Expansion follows evidence reuse. Greenhouses or awnings enter only when the existing property and project graph makes their decisions materially better.

## What should be killed quickly?

Kill or pause a feature when any of these remains true after a defined test window:

- imported pages receive visits but do not create a saved decision or attributable merchant event;
- the recommendation cannot state which retained fact excluded alternatives;
- the category requires expensive tests whose result rarely changes the shortlist;
- feed identity conflicts cannot be resolved economically;
- the merchant's finance, offline or cookie path removes most expected commission;
- installers reject or ignore structured briefs;
- buyers treat the tool as a one-off article and do not return when price or project state changes;
- a channel cannot be measured independently enough to support investment.

The purpose of a stop rule is not pessimism. It keeps capital attached to the mechanism that is actually compounding.
