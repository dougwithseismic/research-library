# The revised founder strategy

## What is the recommendation now?

Keep the premium-garden thesis, but narrow the company that launches.

Do not begin with a broad garden publication and add product tools later. Begin with a **robotic-mower decision system for one property**, then use the same property, evidence and supplier records to expand into connected outdoor projects.

The launch product is:

```text
property and lawn constraints
→ compatible mower shortlist
→ versioned field-test evidence
→ verified offer and price history
→ complete accessory and installation plan
→ retailer checkout or qualified installer brief
→ observed setup and ownership outcome
```

This is a sharper version of the original proposal. Premium garden remains the market; robotic mowers become the first evidence and compatibility wedge.

## Why robotic mowers first?

The initial category has five useful properties.

1. **The decision is genuinely constrained.** Lawn area, slope, zones, narrow passages, trees, boundary technology, connectivity, weather, theft exposure, setup and service change suitability.
2. **The assortment is bounded enough to normalise.** A serious operator can resolve models, generations, packages and required accessories without importing every garden product.
3. **Performance can be observed.** Boundary recovery, missed area, obstacle behaviour, docking, noise, wet operation, setup time and intervention rate can be tested under a published protocol.
4. **A commercial route exists.** Navimow's UK programme states a £1,000-plus average order, 5%-plus commission and a 30-day cookie for qualifying sales. Acceptance, commission groups, attribution and PPC permission remain unknown until confirmed. [Navimow affiliate programme](https://uk.navimow.com/pages/affiliate-program)
5. **Installation creates a second transaction path.** Some buyers can self-install; others need survey, setup or ongoing service. That allows the same decision record to route into retailer or installer economics.

The conservative explicit-purchase phrase `robotic mower for sale` returned only 480 average monthly UK searches in the research ledger. The broad `robot lawn mower` term was much larger but intentionally excluded from commercial totals because intent is mixed. The wedge is therefore not justified by one keyword. It is justified by decision complexity, observable performance, high-value commerce and reuse of the property graph across a larger garden vertical.

## What exactly should version one do?

### Capture a real property

The buyer should be able to store:

- usable lawn area and confidence in that measurement;
- number of separate zones and connecting passages;
- maximum and typical slope;
- tree cover, edges, beds, water and obstacle types;
- available power and docking locations;
- Wi-Fi or mobile coverage where relevant;
- public access, theft and child or pet constraints;
- self-install, assisted setup or full-install preference;
- postcode district for serviceability, without exposing a public address.

Every field needs one of four states: observed by buyer, derived by the system, verified by a supplier, or unknown. That preserves the research standard inside the product.

### Resolve a small canonical catalogue

Begin with approximately 25–40 currently sold UK models across at least four credible manufacturers and several merchants. The exact count follows feed and identity quality; it is not a growth target.

Each record should distinguish:

- manufacturer, family, model and generation;
- nominal and recommended lawn capacity;
- navigation and boundary method;
- slope limits and the conditions attached to them;
- passage and multi-zone requirements;
- included versus optional connectivity;
- included accessories and installation materials;
- warranty, service and repair route;
- merchant offer, delivery and installer availability;
- observed test version and applicable date.

No merchant description becomes a platform fact without a source and field-level status.

### Publish exclusions, not only rankings

The tool should explain why a model has been excluded:

```text
excluded: stated slope limit below property requirement
excluded: multi-zone route not verified
excluded: installation unavailable in postcode district
unknown: narrow-passage performance not yet tested
eligible with condition: additional connectivity module required
```

This is the decision value that a sorted product grid lacks.

### Produce a complete plan

The output includes the mower, base, boundary or reference equipment, connectivity accessories, security, winter storage, expected consumables, installation mode and unresolved site questions. It can be exported as a buyer record and a supplier brief.

## What should the test bench measure?

The first test version should favour repeatable, decision-changing observations over cinematic review production.

| Dimension            | Example observation                                                    | Why it changes the choice                                 |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Setup                | minutes, interventions and required technical skill                    | distinguishes realistic self-install from assisted setup  |
| Boundary handling    | misses, excursions and recovery on defined edge types                  | exposes dependence on ideal lawn geometry                 |
| Narrow passage       | completion and recovery across measured widths                         | determines multi-zone feasibility                         |
| Slope                | ascent, descent, turn and wet-condition behaviour on measured grades   | nominal maximum alone may hide operating limitations      |
| Obstacle handling    | response to defined common objects and fixed obstacles                 | affects safety, intervention and lawn damage              |
| Coverage             | missed-area rate over a defined period                                 | tests whether nominal capacity becomes useful maintenance |
| Intervention         | human resets, rescues and cleaning events per operating hour           | translates autonomy claims into ownership burden          |
| Noise                | measured under a stated method and distance                            | matters in small or boundary-adjacent gardens             |
| Docking and charging | successful returns, charge time and interruptions                      | affects reliability of unattended operation               |
| Service              | parts, support route, response and repairability observed at test date | a high-value product is an ownership decision             |

Version the bench. Retain at least one stable reference course and, where possible, reference units. Publish manufacturer claims separately from measurements. If an attribute has not been tested, mark it unknown rather than converting user reviews into fact.

## How should price and offer history work?

Follow idealo's useful pattern of timestamps and alerts, but attach them to the whole project.

Each offer event stores:

- canonical model and package;
- retailer and deep link;
- observed product price;
- delivery price or unknown;
- included accessories;
- installation included, optional or unavailable;
- stock status exactly as observed;
- warranty and finance route if material;
- observation time and expiry rule;
- affiliate eligibility and commercial status in a separate ledger.

The consumer sees a price range only when compared packages are equivalent. A cheaper bare unit must not outrank a package containing required boundary, reference or installation equipment without showing the difference.

The useful alert can be:

> Tell me when a compatible fully specified setup is under £1,800 and installable in my postcode district.

That retains decision intent rather than sending generic sale emails.

## What should be free and what could be paid?

At launch, the compatibility result, source status, core field-test result and current offer should be free. Hiding the evidence before the brand has trust would weaken acquisition and correction.

Potential later consumer payment must correspond to ongoing utility:

- multiple saved properties or projects;
- long-run price and availability history;
- maintenance and consumables schedule;
- extended test data and comparison exports;
- ownership log and warranty records;
- human review of an unusual site;
- priority concierge or installer coordination.

Membership is not an early assumption. RTINGS and Which demonstrate that it can fund evidence at scale; they do not prove a new garden tool has paid retention.

## How should merchant and installer revenue work?

### Stage 1: CPA or CPS for eligible retail checkout

Use affiliate programmes to validate that the product decision produces payable transactions. Track the actual package, commission group, validation, reversal, time to approval, device path and any finance or offline loss.

Do not optimise the ranking for headline commission. Commercial eligibility can affect whether an offer is displayed, but not whether the product is suitable.

### Stage 2: accepted lead or booked survey for installation

The installer receives the property constraints, selected product class, buyer installation preference, budget, timing and unresolved questions. Payment should attach to an accepted brief or booked survey whose definition is agreed in advance.

A form submission is not a qualified lead merely because it contains a phone number.

### Stage 3: direct merchant packages after proof

Once traffic and outcome records exist, sell bounded commercial inventory:

- enhanced offer data or availability integration;
- sponsored but clearly labelled launch testing that cannot buy the verdict;
- tenancy around a category or season without changing organic suitability;
- higher CPA tied to verified incremental value;
- structured product-data correction or reporting.

### Stage 4: supplier workflow only after repeated demand

If installers repeatedly use the brief, productise status, territory, availability, quote completeness, follow-up and outcome capture. The Auto Trader and Machinio lesson is sequencing: supplier software compounds an active demand network.

## What should the first twelve months look like?

### Months 0–2: prove data and decision structure

- obtain sample product and offer data from at least three merchant routes;
- resolve 25–40 model and package identities manually;
- interview ten recent mower buyers and five installers or retailers;
- define the property schema and exclusion rules;
- publish test-bench version 0.1 before acquiring units;
- verify affiliate and lead-path terms without assuming approval.

**Gate:** stop if product identity or merchant breadth cannot support a neutral shortlist, or if buyer interviews show the selected constraints rarely change the choice.

### Months 3–4: run the evidence pilot

- acquire or legitimately borrow a small comparison set under disclosed terms;
- execute the same course and observation method;
- retain raw measurements, photos and anomalies;
- publish results with source and version status;
- ask manufacturers to correct factual specifications without previewing or buying the verdict.

**Gate:** continue only if the test produces at least three material distinctions not available in merchant feeds and can be repeated at acceptable cost.

### Months 5–6: launch saved projects and offers

- release the property intake, shortlist and exclusion explanation;
- attach comparable offer packages and timestamps;
- let buyers save a threshold and return to a project;
- instrument shortlist completion, exclusion expansion, save, alert, merchant click and downstream validation;
- begin direct, email and partner distribution alongside search.

**Gate:** do not expand the catalogue until a meaningful share of qualified users completes a shortlist or saves a project. The threshold should be set from the first 200 qualified sessions, not invented in advance.

### Months 7–9: validate transactions and installation

- obtain the first 30 attributable eligible sales or accepted installation leads as a learning cohort;
- reconcile headline commission with validated cash;
- measure installer response, acceptance, survey, quote and sale;
- record which property fields prevented wasted contact;
- interview non-converters and returned-product customers where permission exists.

**Gate:** stop paid acquisition or commercial expansion if the actual payable value cannot support evidence, support and acquisition costs under conservative assumptions.

### Months 10–12: choose the expansion path

Only one of three expansions should receive material effort.

1. **Deeper mower ownership:** service, consumables, long-term tests and installer workflow.
2. **Adjacent automation:** irrigation, sensors or selected maintenance systems using the same property record.
3. **Second high-value project:** greenhouse or awning feasibility if the property schema and supplier network materially carry over.

Choose by retained user demand and reusable data, not by keyword volume alone.

## What metrics reveal whether the secret sauce exists?

| Asset            | Leading measure                                      | Stronger proof                                                             |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Product identity | unresolved conflict rate                             | declining manual resolution time without higher error                      |
| Test evidence    | result fields that change eligibility                | observed result predicts fewer returns or interventions                    |
| Compatibility    | shortlist completion and excluded-product inspection | buyer or supplier confirms exclusion prevented a bad choice                |
| Retained intent  | project saves and alert creation                     | return to the same project before purchase                                 |
| Offer history    | equivalent-package coverage and freshness            | alert creates an attributable eligible transaction                         |
| Supplier value   | accepted structured briefs                           | booked survey sale or reduced qualification time                           |
| Distribution     | direct email referral and agent-visible use          | no single channel controls a majority of qualified retained users          |
| Economics        | expected versus validated revenue                    | conservative contribution remains positive after evidence and support cost |

Page views and catalogue size are diagnostic metrics. They do not prove the system.

## What should never be automated without a control?

Automation can propose identity matches, extract specifications, detect anomalies and draft comparison language. It must not silently:

- assert two variants are equivalent;
- convert a manufacturer claim into a measured result;
- infer installer accreditation or insurance;
- invent a price observation or stock event;
- publish a suitability verdict without a traceable rule;
- summarise customer reviews into a fake ownership fact;
- create test imagery for a product that was not tested;
- change commercial ordering without a logged policy.

Every public fact retains source, observation time and status. Every derived eligibility retains the rule version and inputs. Every inference is labelled as such.

## How does the broader garden business eventually form?

The company expands through shared constraints, not a taxonomy.

```text
PROPERTY GRAPH
area • access • slope • power • connectivity • exposure • postcode district
    │
    ├── lawn automation
    ├── greenhouse sizing and placement
    ├── awning feasibility and survey
    ├── outdoor wellness delivery and power
    └── garden building access and installation
```

Each new lane has to reuse at least two existing assets—property data, supplier coverage, evidence method, saved project, offer history or commercial relationship. Otherwise it is a separate business wearing the same logo.

## What would make the revised strategy fail?

The revised strategy should be abandoned or materially changed if:

- buyers do not know or cannot reasonably provide the constraints needed for a reliable shortlist;
- installer survey is always required before useful product-level guidance;
- the bounded test bench costs more to maintain than transactions or membership can plausibly fund;
- model generations and bundles change too quickly for canonical identity to remain current;
- leading merchants withhold feed, attribution or eligible product coverage;
- buyers prefer manufacturer installers so strongly that neutral cross-brand choice adds little;
- saved projects and price thresholds do not create repeat use;
- the outcome remains a generic recommendation that a large agent can produce from public data.

Those are business-model results, not content-performance disappointments.

## Final founder view

The opportunity is still attractive, but the investable unit is smaller and more technical than the original broad publication implies.

Build one category where the company can own product identity, measured performance, compatibility, offer history and a supplier handoff. Prove that those records improve buyer and merchant outcomes. Expand only along constraints already stored in the property graph.

The website will look like a product aggregator. The underlying company should behave like a compact testing lab, a compatibility engine and a project marketplace in that order.
