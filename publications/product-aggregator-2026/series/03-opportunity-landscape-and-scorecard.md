# Opportunity landscape and scorecard

## What survived the first commercial screen?

Four broad verticals survived. A fifth, standalone home wellness, contains high order values but failed the low-CPC requirement and overlaps too heavily with the recommended garden proposition.

| Rank | Vertical                           | Scored commercial searches/month | Weighted CPC | Heuristic score | Confidence  | Decision                              |
| ---: | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------: | -----------: | --------------: | ----------- | ------------------------------------- |
|    1 | Premium garden and outdoor systems |                           27,440 |        £0.94 |          92/100 | High        | Build first                           |
|    2 | Private games and leisure rooms    |                            9,670 |        £0.84 |          82/100 | High        | Cleanest pure affiliate alternative   |
|    3 | Serious maker studios              |                            4,940 |        £0.32 |          79/100 | Medium-high | Best specialist editorial alternative |
|    4 | Land and smallholding equipment    |                           11,850 |        £0.59 |          78/100 | Medium      | Separate marketplace/lead thesis      |
|    5 | Standalone home wellness           |                            2,830 |        £1.63 |          69/100 | Medium      | Do not launch separately              |

The ranking is not determined by search volume alone. Smallholding has more explicit demand than games rooms or maker studios, but its new/used/dealer mixture and weak affiliate-feed fit reduce its score. Maker studios have the smallest surviving demand basket but the cheapest CPC and deepest non-price decision problem.

## Why does premium garden rank first?

Garden is the only screened vertical that combines all of the following:

- two 9,900-search purchase families: greenhouses and hot tubs;
- several further families between 480 and 2,900 searches;
- a product-led basket below £1 weighted CPC;
- documented £1,000-plus robotic mower AOV;
- documented £2,000–£5,000 wellness AOV;
- high-value installed projects adjacent to feed-led products;
- multiple merchant programmes across mowers, wellness and garden buildings;
- a coherent customer project: making the garden easier to manage and more useful.

The biggest strategic advantage is not the 27,440 figure. It is the presence of several independent transaction types under one promise. A mower selector can launch before an awning lead system. A greenhouse comparison can grow without relying on hot-tub content. Outdoor wellness can expand average commission without becoming a standalone wellness publication.

The main risks are seasonality, broad-retailer dilution and the temptation to become a generic garden-products site. One Garden's reported £314 average order is useful negative evidence: the high-ticket thesis depends on category discipline, not the word “garden.” [One Garden programme](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/109432)

## Why do private games and leisure rooms rank second?

Games rooms have stronger direct monetisation proof than any vertical except parts of garden:

- 9,670 canonical commercial searches at £0.84 weighted CPC;
- 4,400 searches for `pool table for sale`;
- 1,300–1,600 each for pinball, arcade machines and jukeboxes;
- a merchant-reported £1,400 AOV and 5% commission;
- at least two active specialist affiliate programmes;
- products with delivery, installation, service and room-planning complexity.

Home Leisure Direct says it frequently sells products around £4,000 and reports £70 commission on an average order. That is strong observed programme evidence, though it remains merchant-authored. [Home Leisure Direct](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/22809)

The score loses points for merchant concentration and independence risk. Specialist retailers already publish buying guides and possess showrooms, technicians and proprietary ranges. A weak aggregator would become a thinner version of those retailers. The winning product must own room planning, independent comparison, serviceability, restored-product knowledge and cross-category packages.

## Why do serious maker studios rank third?

Maker studios have the best raw acquisition profile:

- 4,940 commercial searches at £0.32 weighted CPC;
- 1,900 for `pottery wheel for sale` at £0.22;
- 720 for `pottery kiln for sale` at £0.26;
- 590 for `wood lathe for sale` at £0.18;
- 390 each for milling machines and planer-thicknessers at £0.14 and £0.30;
- live products and bundles above £1,000 and £4,000.

The lower rank reflects demand and merchant uncertainty, not product weakness. Kiln Crafts has an active programme, while Machine Mart brings broad workshop inventory but reports only £100-plus whole-site AOV and 2% commission. Specialist machinery retailers may require direct commercial relationships rather than standard affiliate feeds.

This vertical could outperform its numerical rank if original expertise produces higher conversion and organic authority. Studio buyers face electrical supply, ventilation, dust, floor loading, workflow and compatibility decisions. Those constraints give the publisher something material to know.

## Why is land and smallholding equipment not ranked higher?

Its search economics are exceptional:

| Phrase                            | Searches |                          CPC |
| --------------------------------- | -------: | ---------------------------: |
| mini digger for sale              |    5,400 |                        £0.43 |
| log splitter for sale             |    3,600 |                        £0.77 |
| wood chipper for sale             |    1,900 |                        £0.92 |
| compact tractor for sale          |      480 |                        £0.09 |
| plant and livestock trailer terms |      380 | £0.14 weighted approximately |
| freeze dryer for sale             |       90 |                        £0.67 |

The problem is purchase-path ambiguity. A mini digger can be new, used, imported, dealer-sold, auctioned, financed or hired. Condition, hours, attachments, transport and inspection matter as much as model specification. A retailer-feed comparison covers only a fraction of the decision.

The better model resembles a vertical marketplace:

- dealer inventory feeds;
- private listings;
- lead routing;
- inspection and condition templates;
- transport and finance referrals;
- paid dealer plans;
- saved searches and alerts.

Mini Diggers already sells individual and dealer listing plans, confirming that listing monetisation exists in the category. [Listing plans](https://www.mini-diggers.co.uk/select-your-plan/) The research does not establish the incumbent's transaction volume or profitability.

## Why was standalone home wellness rejected?

The category passes AOV and merchant availability but fails the user's low-CPC priority:

| Phrase                            |  Searches |                CPC |

| ice bath for sale                 |       880 |              £1.61 |
| massage chair price               |       720 |              £1.66 |
| Pilates reformer for sale         |       390 |              £1.06 |
| commercial gym equipment for sale |       320 |              £1.64 |
| infrared sauna for sale           |       210 |              £2.35 |
| commercial treadmill for sale     |       170 |              £1.93 |
| home sauna for sale               |       140 |              £1.60 |
| **Basket**                        | **2,830** | **£1.63 weighted** |

The category is still useful. Outdoor saunas, cold plunges, hot tubs and swim spas belong inside premium garden systems, where they increase order value and editorial breadth. Pilates reformers and massage chairs do not justify a second brand in the first year.

## Which screened ideas did not make the final list?

### Overlanding and camper conversion

Commercial demand was too fragmented:

- `roof tent for sale`: 390 at £0.56;
- `campervan awning for sale`: 170 at £0.51;
- `motorhome awning for sale`: 260 at £0.30;
- `electric outboard motor for sale`: 140 at £0.59, but weakly connected to road overlanding;
- explicit campervan conversion-kit purchase phrases returned no reportable volume.

The broad head terms are much larger, but the explicit commercial basket does not yet support a wide high-AOV publication with the same confidence as the finalists.

### Generic workshop machinery

Workshop equipment works better as a section of serious maker studios than as a general tool catalogue. The latter would inherit thousands of low-value consumables and compete directly with mature retailers. The former can stay focused on machines, studio layout and production workflows.

### Generic garden retail

Generic garden products fail the AOV test. Selections reports a £30 average order across a broad range, while One Garden reports £314. These programmes may add accessory revenue later, but they should not define the business. [Selections](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/5868), [One Garden](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/109432)

### General home improvement

Garden rooms and installed awnings demonstrate why broad home improvement is dangerous. Their CPCs move toward £2–£13 as searches become quote-led, and the buyer/installer relationship becomes local. They belong as a controlled project extension, not as permission to aggregate every renovation service.

## How should the rankings guide a real build?

The ranking answers three different founder objectives:

| Objective                       | Best choice            | Reason                                                                   |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Largest coherent opportunity    | Premium garden systems | Strong demand across several product families and two monetisation lanes |
| Fastest pure affiliate proof    | Private leisure rooms  | Clear AOV, commission and specialist merchant programmes                 |
| Strongest specialist brand moat | Serious maker studios  | Technical decisions, low CPC and high-value studio packages              |
| Highest raw lead-market upside  | Land and smallholding  | Large commercial demand but requires dealer/listing infrastructure       |

The recommended sequence remains garden first. Games rooms and maker studios are credible alternative companies, not categories to bolt onto the same website. Smallholding should be revisited only if the team deliberately chooses a marketplace and dealer-sales business.
